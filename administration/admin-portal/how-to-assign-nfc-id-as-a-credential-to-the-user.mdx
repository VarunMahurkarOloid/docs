Steps to assign NFC/ID:

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Directory** on the left menu panel. ![](/images/8290091/c546ab441e29.png) The **Users** page is displayed.
3. Click user name to edit. ![](/images/8403103/6480148b2457.png) The user details page is displayed. ![](/images/8403103/83f0439c8704.png)
4. Do one of the following:Go to **Credentials** > **NFC/ID**tab> **+** **Assign NFC/ID**. ![](/images/8403103/9a9f5568313a.png) Go to **Assign Credential** > **Assign NFC/ID**. ![](/images/8403103/b691609c12e4.png) The **Assign Badge**pop-up box is displayed. ![](/images/8403103/b9460ab8f97b.png)
5. On the pop-up box, do the following:Enter your unique **NFC/ID**security number.**Note**: You can tap the **NFC/ID**tag on the **Reader** to scan it.Click **Assign**.The NFC/ID security number is assigned and listed on the **NFC/ID** tab. ![](/images/8403103/243686591f01.png)
