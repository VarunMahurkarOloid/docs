Steps to Invite to Add Face in bulk:

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Directory** on the left menu panel. ![Image](/images/8379556/6142e01fd801.png) The **Users**page is displayed.
3. Select **Users.**
4. On top right, Click **Action** > **Send email**> **Invite to add face**. ![Image](/images/8379556/4c5bd91c2a23.png) An email invite link is sent to upload their face. ![](/images/8379556/dabf252cda6e.png) To upload face using the invite link, see [How to add face using the invite link](/administration/user-portal/how-to-add-a-face-using-invite-link-in-oloid-user-portal)
