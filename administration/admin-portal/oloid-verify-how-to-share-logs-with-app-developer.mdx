On the Oloid Verify app, you can share logs with the app developer in two ways:  
​[View and Share Logs with the App Developer](https://oloid.help/en/articles/13406491-oloid-verify-how-to-share-logs-with-app-developer)  
​[Upload Logs to the App Developer](https://oloid.help/en/articles/13406491-oloid-verify-how-to-share-logs-with-app-developer)

## Prerequisite

Endpoint configuration for Debug Logs should be enabled in the Tenant Admin Portal. For more information, refer to [How to Enable Debug Logs](/administration/admin-portal/oloid-verify-how-to-configure-endpoint-in-tenant-admin-portal)

## View and Share Logs with the App Developer

## Steps to View and Share Logs

1. Launch the **Oloid Verify** app. **Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the preview screen of the App. For more details, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Verify endpoint](/passwordless/verify/oloid-verify-how-to-pair-an-endpoint)
2. Tap the screen four times using a single finger. ![](/images/8649904/ac70a29f57d6.png) The **OnDevice Sync Report** pop-up box is displayed.
3. Tap **VIEW LOGS.** ![](/images/8649904/45a73ba7287d.png) The log report is displayed on the screen.
4. On the top right corner of the report page, tap share button to send the log report to the developer. ![](/images/8649904/cdff85a66ccb.png) **Note:**use **AirDrop** to transfer the logs to another iPad or iOS device where the Mail app is configured. Once transferred, you can email the logs from that device. ![](/images/8649904/c92bb6e8155e.png)

## Upload Logs to the App Developer

## Steps to Upload Logs

1. Launch the **Oloid Verify App**.**Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the preview screen of the App. For more details, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Verify endpoint](/passwordless/verify/oloid-verify-how-to-pair-an-endpoint) ​ ![](/images/8649904/ac70a29f57d6.png)
2. Double tap with two fingers on the preview screen to launch the Admin Mode.
3. Tap **YES-CONTINUE.** ![](/images/8649904/504a58c23777.png) The **Settings**screen is displayed.
4. Tap **Start** in the **Health Check** section. ![](/images/8649904/7ea7fcfe1496.png) The **Please Select Health Type** pop-up box is displayed.
5. Tap **Upload Logs.** ![](/images/8649904/01d0a6e5e1ba.png) The logs are successfully uploaded and automatically shared with the app developer. ![](/images/13405850/8ef58ea6c938.png)
