## Introduction

This document provides instructions to delete a group from the Tenant Admin Portal.

## Prerequisite

You must have admin access to the Tenant Admin Portal.

## Steps to Delete a Group

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page, click **Groups** on the left menu panel. ![](/images/8493877/1f65ec6ced12.png) The **Groups** page is displayed.
3. Click the preferred group. ![](/images/8493877/7364a7df260c.png) The Group details page is displayed.
4. Go to **Action** drop-down and select **Delete**. ![](/images/8493877/87fbdca7fc07.png) The confirmation pop-up box is displayed.
5. DO the following:Enter the **Group** **Name**.Enter the **reason** to delete the group.Click **Delete**. ![](/images/8493877/1bb1f53826b3.png) The group is deleted successfully. ![](/images/8493877/bb39d6ce59c8.png)
