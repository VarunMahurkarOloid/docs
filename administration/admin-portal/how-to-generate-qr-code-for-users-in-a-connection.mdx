Steps to generate QR Code for a Connection:

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Directory**on the left menu panel. ![Image](/images/8385745/6142e01fd801.png) The **Users** page is displayed. ![](/images/8385745/f515cbacc04d.png)
3. Go to **Action**> **Generate QR Code Credential**. ![Image](/images/8385745/2b755421ffd4.png) The **Generate QR Code** pop-up box is displayed. ![](/images/8385745/7a63ae1d8d41.png)
4. On the pop-up box, do the following:Select the connection from the **Connection** dropdown list.Under **QR Code Validity** field adjust the date and time from the calendar icon.Under **Share Summary With**field, enter the email ID(s) with whom you want to share the QR Code summary. (**optional**)Enable **Send QR Code** to get the generated QR codes with summary. (**optional**)Click **Assign**.The QR Code credentials are successfully assigned to users in the selected connection. The QR Code summary of these users is shared over email ID(s) and QR codes areis available for download in the attachments. ![](/images/8385745/af07b11a3fd2.png)
