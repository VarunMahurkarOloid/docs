## Introduction

This document provides instructions to sync users from a group to the associated endpoint devices in the Tenant Admin Portal.

## Prerequisite

You must have admin access to the Tenant Admin Portal.

## Steps to Sync User

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page, click **Groups** on the left panel menu. ![](/images/8462702/1f65ec6ced12.png) The **Groups**page is displayed.
3. Click the preferred **Group Name**. ![](/images/8462702/9e4153afd099.png) The **Group Details** page is displayed. ![](/images/8462702/49362e6f3297.png)
4. Do one of the following:Go to **Action**> click **Sync Users**. ![](/images/8462702/11b78a3ba0d6.png) The**Confirm Action** pop-up box is displayed.Click **Yes**. ![](/images/8462702/f12493dddc65.png) The users in the group are synced to the associated device(s) successfully. ![](/images/8462702/1a4809862087.png) ORGo to **Users**tab > click **Sync** icon. ![](/images/8462702/9302c5e0160e.png) The users in the group are synced to the associated endpoint device(s) successfully. ![](/images/8462702/1a4809862087.png)
