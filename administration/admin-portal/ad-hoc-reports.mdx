## Introduction

Ad-hoc reports, also referred to as Prebuilt reports can be created, saved, and downloaded in the Oloid Portal. Pre-built reports are of 8 types:

[Endpoint Details Report](#h_6701cddd46)

[Endpoint Status Report](#h_c9892f4f4a)

[Transaction Report](#h_56affeaee8)

[Users Report](#h_ef30788776)

[Supervisors Report](#h_c17bbeee96)

[User Transactions Report](#h_485ed6d0ac)

[Endpoint Update Log](#h_ca2bc189f0)

[Endpoint Login Log](#h_24064dc9f5)

## Pre-requisite

You must have a Tenant Admin account.

## Endpoint Details Report

An Endpoint Details Report provides detailed information on all the endpoints associated with the application.

Here's how to create and download Endpoint Details reports:

1. Open the Oloid Portal.
2. Navigate to "**Reports**" > "**Ad-Hoc**".
3. Click "**Create & Download Report**."
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to create & download report.For detailed instructions, see [How to create & download Endpoint details report](/administration/admin-portal/how-to-create-and-download-endpoint-details-report).
