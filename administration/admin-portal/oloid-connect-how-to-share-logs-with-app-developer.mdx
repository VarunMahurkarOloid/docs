On the **Oloid Connect** app, you can share logs with the app developer in two ways:

[View and Share App Logs with the Developer](#h_5969a27f19)  
​[Upload Logs to the App Developer](#h_eba6e2b1ba)

## Prerequisite

Endpoint configuration for Debug Logs should be enabled in the Tenant Admin Portal. For more information, refer to [How to Enable Debug Logs](/administration/admin-portal/oloid-connect-how-to-configure-endpoint-in-the-tenant-admin-portal)

## View and Share Logs with the App Developer

## Steps to View and Share Logs

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect App**. **Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the initial screen of the App. For more, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint).
2. Tap the screen four times using a single finger. ​ ![](/images/10308108/bdb26aa81cf8.png) The **OnDevice Sync Report** pop-up box is displayed.
3. Tap **View Logs.** ![](/images/13405850/571f26048218.png) The log report is displayed on the screen.
4. On the top right corner of the report page, tap share button to send the log report to the developer. ![](/images/13405850/526ca1fd5c28.png) **Note:**use **AirDrop** to transfer the logs to another iPad or iOS device where the Mail app is configured. Once transferred, you can email the logs from that device. ![](/images/13405850/755ea537431b.png)

## Upload Logs to the App Developer

## Steps to Upload Logs

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect App**.**Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the preview screen of the App. For more details, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint). ​ ![](/images/10308108/bdb26aa81cf8.png)
2. Double tap with two fingers on the preview screen to launch the Admin Mode.
3. Tap **Yes- Continue.** ![](/images/10308108/a2078cd1bdd8.png) The **Settings**screen is displayed.
4. Tap **Start** in the **Health Check** section. ![](/images/13405850/6a59b273337d.png) The **Please Select Health Type** pop-up box is displayed.
5. Tap **Upload Logs.** ![](/images/13405850/df9f423a8af2.png) The logs are successfully uploaded and automatically shared with the app developer. ![](/images/13405850/8ef58ea6c938.png)
