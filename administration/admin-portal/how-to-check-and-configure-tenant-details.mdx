### Introduction

The Tenant Admin Portal allows admin to view and configure tenant details. In the portal, you can access tenant information such as tenant name, owner details, and company information.

### Steps to view Tenant details:

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the homepage of the portal, click **user profile icon**on the bottom left corner of the menu panel. ![](/images/8810967/909bc3357212.png) The user profile menu is displayed.
3. Click **Tenant** **Settings**. ![](/images/8810967/e184a5672785.png) The **Tenant Settings** page is displayed. ![](/images/8810967/504fe5791aec.png)
4. Do the following under the Tenant**Details** tab: View **Tenant Name**.View **Tenant Owner Email**.View/Edit **Tenant Owner Name**.View/Edit **Company Name**Enter **Customer Address**. (Optional)Click **Save**. The Tenant Details are saved.
