Steps to Delete Endpoint

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Endpoints** on the left menu panel.![](/images/11196020/37e34703983c.png)The Endpoints page is displayed.
3. Select**Endpoint Name**to edit.![](/images/8463435/36867f4d7e2c.png)The Endpointdetail page is displayed.
4. Go to **Action**dropdown and select **Delete**.![](/images/8463435/2928e0233b92.png)A pop-up box is displayed to confirm your action.![](/images/8463435/a0fe3900e94c.png)
5. On the pop-up box, click **Delete**.The endpoint is permanently deleted.
