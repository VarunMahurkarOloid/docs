## Introduction

The **Tenant Admin Portal** serves as the management portal for creating and managing users, applications, connections, groups, and credentials within the Oloid system. It enables administrators to configure authentication methods, integrate with identity providers, monitor system activity through reports, and perform other critical administrative tasks.

## Prerequisite

You should be a user with Tenant Admin role.

## Steps to log in to the Tenant Admin Portal

1. Launch your browser.
2. Enter the URL provided for you in the browser’s address bar > press **Enter** on your keyboard. The Tenant Admin Portal **Login**page is displayed.
3. Enter your **Username**or **Email**.
4. Enter Password > Click **Login**.**Note**: Oloid uses **SSO login** and also allows you to log in to the Tenant Admin Portal with your [Google](/administration/admin-portal/tenant-admin-portal-how-to-log-in-to-tenant-admin-portal-with-google-sso), [Microsoft](/administration/admin-portal/tenant-admin-portal-how-to-log-in-to-tenant-admin-portal-with-microsoft-sso), [Okta](/resources/integrations/sso/how-to-log-in-to-the-tenant-admin-portal-with-okta-sso), [PingOne](/resources/integrations/sso/how-to-log-in-to-oloid-portal-with-pingone-sso), [Auth0](/administration/admin-portal/how-to-log-in-to-tenant-admin-portal-using-auth0-sso)or with your Email account. ![](/images/8462765/69b6e904d457.png) You are logged in to the Tenant Admin Portal successfully. ![](/images/8462765/aacdd8e0e9c6.png)
