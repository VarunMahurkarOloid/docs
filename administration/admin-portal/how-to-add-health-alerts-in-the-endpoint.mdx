Steps to add health alerts:

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the homepage of the portal, click **Endpoints**on the left menu panel. ![](/images/8245679/7658c3424e49.png) The Endpoints page is displayed.
3. Click Endpoint Name to edit.![](/images/8227103/74805360adfe.png)The Endpoint detail page is displayed.
4. On the right panel of the endpoint detail page, locate **Health**section.![](/images/8245679/a5229b8d23a3.png)
5. Do the followingClick **Check Health**to check the health status of the endpoint.Enter the recipient's email id(s) in **Send Health Alerts to** field.Set**Email frequency**in Hours and Mins to adjust the frequency of email notifications.Turn on **Enable Heath Alerts** toggle button to receive health alerts of the endpoint at your configured email.Click **Save**. The health alert setup is configured. The email recipient receives the health alerts based on the alert frequency. ​
