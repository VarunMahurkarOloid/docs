## Steps to create CloudKey

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the homepage of the portal, click **Directory** on the left panel menu. ![](/images/8290091/c546ab441e29.png) The **Users**page is displayed.
3. Click **user name** to edit. ![](/images/8290091/6edb46330780.png) The user details page is displayed.
4. Do one of the following:Go to**Credentials**> **Cloud Key**> **+ Create CloudKey**. ![](/images/8290091/5487f0850c65.png) Go to **Assign Credential**> **Create CloudKey**. ![](/images/8290091/3c24221f7756.png) The **Create CloudKey** pop-up box is displayed. ![](/images/8290091/b9b2f31d706e.png)
5. Enter **Start Date / Time** and **End Date/Time.****Note**: You can click **Now**to set the current date and time. The end date is automatically set to 1 year from the current date.You can also click **10 years from Now**to set the **End Date/Time**to 10 years from the current date/time.
6. Click **Create**.The cloudkey is created and listed on the **Cloud Key** tab. ![](/images/8290091/ee31f5988aeb.png)
