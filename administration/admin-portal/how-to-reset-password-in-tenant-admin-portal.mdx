Resetting your password in the Tenant Admin Portal allows you to create a new password if you've forgotten the current one or need to update it for security. The process includes verifying your identity, receiving a reset link or code, setting a new password, and logging in securely.

**Pre-requisite**

- You should be a user in the Tenant Admin Portal with **Admin**role.

**Follow the below steps to reset password :**

1. Launch your browser.
2. Enter the URL provided for you in the browser’s address bar > press **Enter** on your keyboard.The Oloid LOGIN page is displayed.
3. Click **Forgot Password.**![](/images/11411467/6e2b46c886e2.png) The reset password pop-up box is displayed.
4. Do any one of the following:Enter your registered **Email ID**.ORSelect **Country**from drop down menu > Enter your registered **phone number**.
5. Click **Reset**. ![](/images/11411467/6ce6b2cc33d5.png) A reset password email is sent to the provided email ID.![](/images/11411467/9ccf0dd8f0d2.png)
6. Go to your email **inbox >**open the **Oloid Password Reset**email.
7. Click **Reset Password**link. ![](/images/11411467/cc5a5fd533f9.png) The Set New Password pop-up box is displayed.
8. Enter your **Email ID**.
9. Enter **Confirmation Code** received on the same email.
10. Enter **New Password**.
11. Click **Login**. ![](/images/11411467/735cdff3ea71.png) The password is successfully reset in the Tenant Admin Portal. The Tenant Admin Portal home page is displayed. ![](/images/11411467/1b60660de039.png)

**Note:**  
If you are unable to reset your password using the above steps, you may also contact your **Support team** to request a reset password link. Once received, click the link and follow the on-screen instructions to complete the reset process.
