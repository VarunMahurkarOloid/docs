**Pre-requisite**

- You should be a user with Tenant Admin role in Oloid Portal.
- A user account must be created in the Auth0 dashboard.

**Steps to log in to Tenant Admin Portal using Auth0 SSO Login option**:

1. Launch your browser.
2. Enter the URL provided for you in the browser’s address bar. Then, press **Enter** on your keyboard.The **Oloid Portal** page is displayed.
3. Click **Log in with SSO**. ![](/images/11130515/3e1b0545e098.png) The configured Login options are displayed.
4. Click**Continue with Auth0**. ![](/images/11130515/027893e02bdd.png) The **Welcome** pop-up is displayed.
5. Do the following:Enter your**Email address**.Enter your **Password**.
6. Click **Continue**. ![](/images/11130515/a822d9b20970.png) You are logged in to the Tenant Admin Portal using Auth0 SSO successfully. ![](/images/11130515/aacdd8e0e9c6.png)
