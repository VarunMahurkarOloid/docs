Steps to send invite to download app:

1. Login to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the homepage, click **Directory** from the left menu panel. ![Image](/images/9929232/bfbd42eb3ee2.png) The **Users** page is displayed.
3. Click **username** to edit. ![Image](/images/9929232/d626c453e0da.png) The **User details** page is displayed. ![](/images/9929232/83f0439c8704.png)
4. Go to **Credentials** tab > **Bluetooth** > **Invite to Download app**. ![Image](/images/9929232/01008a9a0a1f.png) The invite to download app is sent successfully to the user. ![](/images/9929232/95179d747b07.png)
