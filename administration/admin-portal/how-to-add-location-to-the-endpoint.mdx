Steps to add Location to the Endpoint:

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Endpoints** on the left panel menu. ![Image](/images/8223212/07d606cf41a5.png) The **Endpoints** page is displayed.
3. Click **Endpoint name** to edit. ![Image](/images/8223212/6145abb1f798.png) The **Endpoint detail** page is displayed. ![](/images/8223212/3efead364a92.png)
4. On the bottom right corner, locate **Endpoint Location**. ![](/images/8223212/c938f0887287.png)
5. Do the following:Select an **Address** from the dropdown list.**Note**: You can also fill in the address field on your own.Select **Location** from dropdown list.Select **Building** fromdropdown list.Select area from **Area**dropdown.Click **Save**. The endpoint address is successfully added to the endpoint detail page. ![Image](/images/8223212/777add229086.png)
