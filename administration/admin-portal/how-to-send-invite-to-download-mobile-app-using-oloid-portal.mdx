Steps to send invite to download mobile app:

1. Log in to **Oloid Portal.**
2. On the homepage, click **Directory**from the left menu panel. ![Image](/images/9716548/3bb8e0636a91.png) The**Users** page is displayed. ![](/images/9716548/1f4a03b00f6b.png)
3. Select user(s).
4. Click **Action** > **Send Email** > **Invite to download mobile app.** ![Image](/images/9716548/9bab43812bba.png) The invite to download mobile app is sent successfully.
