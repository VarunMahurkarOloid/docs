Steps to generate QR Code :

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Directory**on the left menu panel. ![Image](/images/8469163/6142e01fd801.png) The **Users** page is displayed.
3. Select user(s) to generate QR Code. ![Image](/images/8469163/185bf7ebab4b.png)
4. Go to **Action > Generate QR Code Credential**. ![Image](/images/8469163/d9979b0253ef.png) The **Generate QR Code** pop-up box is displayed. ![](/images/8469163/ff35c3fed4ed.png)
5. Do the following:Under **QR Code Validity** field adjust the date and time from the calendar icon.Under **Share Summary With**field enter the email ID(s) with whom you want to share the QR Code summary.(**optional**)Enable **Send QR Code** to get the generated QR codes with summary. (**optional**)Click **Assign**.The QR Code credential is successfully assigned to the users. The QR Code summary of these users is shared over email ID(s) and is available for download in the attachments. ![](/images/8469163/ea57bb640121.png)
