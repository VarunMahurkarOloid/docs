Steps to create Legal Document:

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Consent Documents** on the left menu panel.![](/images/8329688/1c7dc51a902e.png)The **Consent Documents** page is displayed.
3. On the **Consent Documents** page, click **Legal Documents**tab.![](/images/8329688/221ba1491914.png)
4. Click **Create Legal Document**.![](/images/8329688/55549d0827c2.png)The **Upload Legal Document**pop-up box is displayed.![](/images/8329688/2bb592bec110.png)
5. On the pop-up box, do the following:Click to browse the file or drag and drop the file to upload. Enter **Document Title**. Enter **Version**. Enter **Language**.Click **Upload**.

The document is uploaded and listed on the **Consent Documents** page.
