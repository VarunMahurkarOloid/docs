**Steps to configure GSuite SSO:**

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the homepage of the portal, click **user profile icon**on the bottom left corner of the menu panel. ![](/images/10299914/c8560f399688.png) The user profile menu is displayed.
3. Click **Tenant** **Settings**. ![Image](/images/10299914/dd6256eec1ca.png) The **Tenant Settings** page is displayed.
4. Go to **Security**> click **GSuite**. ![](/images/11129566/a3e708787929.png)
5. Turn on **Enable GSuite SSO**toggle > click **Save**. ![](/images/11129566/7d49afc1b0fe.png) The **GSuite** SSO configuration is saved successfully. ![](/images/11129566/d46fe8827dc1.png)
