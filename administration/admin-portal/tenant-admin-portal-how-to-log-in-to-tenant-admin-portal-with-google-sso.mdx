## Introduction

This document explains how to log in to the Oloid Tenant Admin Portal using Google Single Sign-On (SSO).

## Prerequisites

- You should be a user in the Tenant Admin Portal with **Admin/Supervisor** role.
- The Google credential (user email ID) must be added to the user detail page.

## Steps to log into Tenant Admin Portal using **Google SSO**

1. Launch your browser.
2. Enter the URL provided for you in the browser’s address bar > press **Enter** on your keyboard.The Oloid LOGIN page is displayed.
3. Click **Login with SSO.** ![](/images/8727486/8be0bc89f301.png)
4. Click **Continue with Google**to log in to Oloid Platform with Google SSO.The configured Log in options are displayed. ![](/images/8727486/3cdf0eac8cca.png) The Sign in pop-up box is displayed.
5. Enter your **Email ID** > click **Next**. ![](/images/8727486/f2c9e31b236c.png)
6. Enter your **Password** > click **Next**.

You are logged in to the Tenant Admin Portal using Google SSO successfully.
