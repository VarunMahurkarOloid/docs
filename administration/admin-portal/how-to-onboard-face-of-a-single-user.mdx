Steps to add face of a single user:

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Directory** on the left menu panel. ![](/images/8181773/c15cff27bade.png) The **Users**page is displayed. ![Image](/images/8181773/f5532b8335bf.png)
3. Click **username** to edit. ![Image](/images/8181773/d626c453e0da.png) The user details page is displayed. ![](/images/8181773/83f0439c8704.png)
4. Do one of the following:Go to**Credentials**> **Face**> + **Add Face**. ![](/images/8181773/17183ad5cea1.png) Go to **Assign Credential**> **Add Face**. ![](/images/8181773/0bc81a98576d.png) The Add Face pop-up box to upload image is displayed.
5. Click or Drag and Drop an image to be uploaded. ![](/images/8181773/b2448a45a2cc.png)
6. Click **Upload**. ![](/images/8181773/f3bbfb589cbc.png) The image is uploaded and listed on the **Face** tab.**Note**: You can add a maximum of five faces per user.
