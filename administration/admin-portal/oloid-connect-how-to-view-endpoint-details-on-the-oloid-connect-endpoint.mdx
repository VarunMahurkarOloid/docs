## Pre-requisite:

The endpoint must be paired.

For more, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint)

## Steps to view Endpoint Details on the Endpoint:

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect**![](/images/10288412/f7c4703dd471.png) **App**.**Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the initial screen of the App. For more, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint). ​ ![](/images/8616263/bdb26aa81cf8.png)
2. Double tap with two fingers on the initial screen to launch the Admin Mode.
3. Tap **Yes- Continue.** ![](/images/8616263/a2078cd1bdd8.png) The **Settings**screen is displayed.
4. On the Settings page, the endpoint details are displayed. ![](/images/8616263/cc9164f87bdf.png)
