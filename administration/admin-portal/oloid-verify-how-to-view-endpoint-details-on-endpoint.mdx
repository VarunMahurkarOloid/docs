## Introduction

The View Endpoint Details feature in the Oloid Verify App allows Tenant Admin and Supervisor to quickly access essential information about the paired endpoint directly from the device.

## Prerequisite

You must pair the Oloid Verify endpoint. To pair, refer **[How to pair an Oloid Verify endpoint](/passwordless/verify/oloid-verify-how-to-pair-an-endpoint).**

## Steps to to view endpoint details

1. Launch the **Oloid Verify**App. ![](/images/8616247/65448eab37c7.png) **Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the preview screen of the App. For more information, refer to [How to Pair an Oloid Verify Endpoint](/passwordless/verify/oloid-verify-how-to-pair-an-endpoint) ![](/images/8616247/ac70a29f57d6.png)
2. Double tap with two fingers on the preview screen to launch the Admin Mode.
3. Tap **Yes- Continue.** ![](/images/8616247/504a58c23777.png) The **Settings**screen along with endpoint details are displayed. ![](/images/8616247/7da76e3219d0.png) **Note**: Endpoint details can also be viewed in the **Settings tab** after logging in as an Admin in Oloid Verify app. To log in, refer to [How to log in to the admin mode of Oloid Verify App as an Admin or Supervisor](/administration/admin-portal/oloid-verify-how-to-log-in-to-admin-mode-as-an-admin-or-supervisor). ![](/images/8616247/10668ab34974.png) SEO KeywordsOloid Verify view endpoint details | Check endpoint details Oloid Verify | Oloid Verify endpoint information
