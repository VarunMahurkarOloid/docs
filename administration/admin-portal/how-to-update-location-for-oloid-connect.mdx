### Steps to update location:

1. Go to **Settings** on **iPad**. ![Image](/images/10204617/c64967cca550.png) The Settings menu is displayed. ![](/images/10288412/16df6d6a1ee9.png)
2. Search **Oloid Connect** ![](/images/10288412/f7c4703dd471.png) app and open. ![](/images/10288412/5a9b282950a4.png) The **Oloid Connect** app menu is displayed.
3. Tap **Location**. ![](/images/10288412/ce934d0620f0.png) The **Allow Location Access** menu is displayed.
4. Do one of the following:**Never:** The app cannot use your location, even when you’re using the app.**Ask Next Time Or When I Share**: Every time you open the app, it’ll ask to use your location.**While Using the App:**The app can use your location only when you're using that app.
5. Enable **Precise Location** to allow the app to detect your specific location.**Note:** Precise Location option appears when you allow location access.The location is updated successfully. ![](/images/10288412/42c9b69d2c1e.png)
