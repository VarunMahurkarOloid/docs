Steps to send QR code:

1. Login to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the homepage, click **Directory** from the left menu panel. ![Image](/images/9794751/bfbd42eb3ee2.png) The **Users** page is Displayed.
3. Click **username** to edit. ![Image](/images/9794751/d626c453e0da.png) The **User details** page is displayed. ![](/images/9794751/83f0439c8704.png)
4. Go to **Credentials** tab > **QR Code** > **Send QR Code on Email**. ![Image](/images/9794751/26d2365503b4.png) The **Send QR Code on Email** pop-up box is displayed. ![](/images/9794751/0edea1901376.png)
5. On the pop-up screen, do the following :Enter/Select Email ID from the dropdown list to whom you want to **Share QR Code With**.Click **Send**. The QR Code is sent successfully to the email ID. ![](/images/9794751/2986cf9698d8.png) Video TutorialWatch the video [How to send QR Code on email in the Tenant Admin Portal - Video Tutorial](/administration/admin-portal/how-to-send-qr-code-on-email-in-the-tenant-admin-portal-video-tutorial)
