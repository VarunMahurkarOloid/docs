Steps to enroll user using badge scan:

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Directory** on the left menu panel. ![Image](/images/8937141/6142e01fd801.png) The **Users**page is displayed.
3. Click user name to edit. ![Image](/images/8937141/d626c453e0da.png) The **user details** page is displayed. ![](/images/8937141/83f0439c8704.png)
4. Go to**Credentials**tab > **Badge**> **+** **Scan Badge to Enroll**. ![Image](/images/8937141/b86bb34b98fa.png) The **Scan your Badge to Enroll** pop-up box is displayed. ![](/images/8937141/f2b585cfe6d0.png)
5. On the pop-up box, do the following:Select the badge setting as configured in the badge reader.Enter your badge ID by scanning your badge or manually type it in the **Scan or Type Data**field.**Note**: To scan the badge, card reader should be connected to your device.Enter the bits in the **Number of Bits**field. (Optional)Click **Save**.The badge is assigned to the user and listed on the badge tab. ![](/images/8937141/a41d41e49663.png)
