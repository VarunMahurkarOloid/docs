Steps to Deactivate OloID Account of an User:

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Directory** on the left panel menu. ![Image](/images/8394599/6142e01fd801.png) The **Users** page is displayed.
3. Click **Username** to edit. ![Image](/images/8394599/02895dcd6f77.png) The user details page is displayed.
4. On the top-right, go to **Action** > **Deactivate Oloid Account**. ![Image](/images/8394599/188a1472545b.png) A confirmation pop-up box is displayed. ![](/images/8394599/37ac0186be4c.png)
5. Select **Block**.The user's Oloid account is removed successfully. ![](/images/8394599/967837977a12.png) **Note**: To recover your account you must create Oloid account.For more information, see**[How to create OloID account](/administration/admin-portal/how-to-create-oloid-account-in-tenant-admin-portal)**.
