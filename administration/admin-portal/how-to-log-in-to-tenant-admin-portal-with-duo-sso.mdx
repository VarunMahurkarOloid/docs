## Introduction

This document explains how to log in to the Tenant Admin Portal using Duo SSO. It includes step-by-step instructions for completing the login process with Duo authentication.

Before you begin, make sure Duo SSO is enabled for your account. To enable it, submit a request to [Oloid Support.](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) Activation may take up to 48 hours.

## ​**Prerequisites**

Before logging into the Tenant Admin Portal using Duo SSO, ensure the following:

- You are a registered user in the **Tenant Admin Portal**with the Admin role.
- Duo SSO is **enabled for your account**. If not, submit a request to **Oloid Support** for activation, which may take up to 48 hours.
- You have the **login URL** provided to access the Tenant Admin Portal.

## Steps to log into Tenant Admin Portal using **Duo SSO**

1. Launch your browser.
2. Enter the provided **URL** in the browser’s address bar and press **Enter**. The **Oloid Login**page is displayed.
3. Click **Login with SSO**.![](/images/12147243/d38d3fb7a8c4.png)The configured Log in options are displayed.
4. Click **Continue with Duo**to log in to Oloid Platform with Duo SSO.![](/images/12147243/753481890e0e.png)The Single Sign-On screen is displayed.
5. Follow below steps to complete the sign-in process.a. Enter your**Email address,**then click **Next**.![A screenshot of a login form AI-generated content may be incorrect.](/images/12147243/0d5a12c0bab6.png)b. Enter your **Password**, then click **Log in**.![](/images/12147243/2d5120996869.png)The **Duo authenticator**screen is displayed.**Note:** To update your email address, click **edit**. This will take you to the login screen where you can update the email address. c. On authenticator screen, select your preferred authentication method.**Note:** The available authentication method may vary depending on how you configured Duo when setting up your device in the **Duo Consent app**.**For Example:** If you choose **Duo Mobile passcode,** a code will appear in the app. For **Text Message** or **Duo Push**, you will receive a message or push notification.![](/images/12147243/0f303ec8fcc8.png)

On successful authentication, you are logged in to the Oloid User Portal using Duo SSO.
