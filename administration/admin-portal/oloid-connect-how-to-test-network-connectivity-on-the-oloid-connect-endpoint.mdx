### Steps to test network connectivity on the Endpoint:

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect**![](/images/10288412/f7c4703dd471.png) **App**.**Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the initial screen of the App. For more, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint). ​ ![](/images/10308108/bdb26aa81cf8.png)
2. Double tap with two fingers on the initial screen to launch the Admin Mode.
3. Tap **Yes- Continue.** ![](/images/10308108/a2078cd1bdd8.png) The **Settings**screen is displayed.
4. Tap **Start** in the **Health Check** section. ![](/images/13405850/6a59b273337d.png) The **Please Select Health Type** pop-up box is displayed.
5. On the pop-up box, tap **Network Route Status**.
