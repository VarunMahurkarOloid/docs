## Introduction

This document explains how to configure Screensaver settings for the Oloid Connect application in the Tenant Admin Portal. The configured screensaver is displayed on the application's initial screen.

## Prerequisites

- You must have access to Tenant Admin Portal.
- The **Oloid Connect application** must be created and configured in the Tenant Admin Portal. For more, refer to [How to create and configure Oloid Connect Application in Tenant Admin Portal](/administration/admin-portal/how-to-create-and-configure-oloid-connect-application-in-tenant-admin-portal)

## Steps to Configure Screensaver

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the **Applications** page, click on the preferred Oloid Connect application. ![](/images/13212909/ab8cc0504bc4.png) The application **details** page is displayed. ![](/images/13212909/9ae92c6b0482.png)
3. Go to **Theme** tab > Enable **Screensaver** toggle. **Note:** When enabled, all screensaver configuration fields become active. ![](/images/14493925/301b2899c62a.png)
4. Under **Select Mode**, select one of the following:Select**Passive Wake Mode** to show a cover screen while keeping the camera on in the background. The cover screen closes automatically when a face is detected. [See Sample screen](#h_33c42f0ebf)Select **Energy Saver Mode** to turn off the camera and show **Resume** button on the screen. Tap on the **Resume** button to turn on the camera again and continue authentication. [See Sample screen](#h_f63be37315)
5. Under **Trigger Screen-saver after**, set how long the device waits before the selected mode starts. Select the required time in minutes (**Min**) and seconds (**Sec**). ![](/images/14493925/2100e47328f4.png)
6. Under **Customize Screensaver**, do the following:Enter **Title**. Enter **Subtitle** (optional).Enter **Text Color** using a hex code.
7. Upload images as required:**Customer Logo** – Click or drag an image to upload.**Note:** The **Customer Logo** must meet the required specifications. It should be up to **528 × 144 px**, with a maximum size of **1 MB**, and must be in **SVG or PNG format**.**Background Image** – Click or drag an image to upload.**Note:** The **Background Image** must be up to **1080 × 1920 px** with a maximum size of **2 MB**, and must be in **SVG or PNG format**. ![](/images/14493925/cfbc676be0b7.png)
8. Click **Deploy Changes**. ![](/images/14493925/9482f0030107.png) The screensaver is enabled and configured successfully. ![](/images/14493925/7d0b50723dcc.png) Sample screen for Passive Wake Mode![](/images/14493925/3595c3cba1cf.png) **Note:** Tap the screen or place your face in front of the camera to turn on camera and continue authentication. Sample screen for Energy Saver Mode ![](/images/14493925/731c1fb53aff.png) **Note:** Tap on the **Resume** button or place your face in front of the camera to turn on the camera again and continue authentication.
