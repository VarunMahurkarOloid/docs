A Tenant Admin and Supervisor can log in to the admin mode of the Oloid Connect App through the following methods:

- [Log in Using Email and Password](#h_4c58c51d32)
- [Log in Using Face](#h_c2400bc029)
- [Log in Using SSO](#h_6a328cd429)

## Log in Using Email and Password

## Pre-requisite:

- The user must be a Tenant Admin or Supervisor to log in to Admin Mode.
- The endpoint must be paired.For more, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint)

### Steps to log using Email and Password :

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect**![](/images/10288412/f7c4703dd471.png)**App**.**Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the preview screen of the App. For more, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint). ![](/images/8671144/bdb26aa81cf8.png)
2. Double tap with two fingers on the preview screen to launch the Admin Mode.
3. Tap **Yes- Continue.** ![](/images/8616263/a2078cd1bdd8.png) The **Settings**screen is displayed.
4. Click **Login as Admin**. ![](/images/8671144/3b700b8df0b1.png)The Login screen is displayed.
5. Do the following:Enter your email ID in the **Email** text field.Enter your password in the **Password** text field.
6. Tap **Login.** ![](/images/8671144/ddc752988d4e.png) You are logged in to the Admin Mode successfully.

## Log in Using Face

**Pre-Requisite:**

- Face should be onboarded for the Tenant Admin or Supervisor.
- Endpoint should be paired with the Oloid Connect application.
- The Connection assigned to the Supervisor must be linked to the application paired with the endpoint. For further details, refer [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint)

### Steps to log in with Face:

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect**![](/images/10288412/f7c4703dd471.png)**App**.**Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the preview screen of the App. For more, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint). ![](/images/8671144/bdb26aa81cf8.png)
2. Double tap with two fingers on the preview screen to launch the Admin Mode.
3. Tap **Yes- Continue.** ![](/images/8616263/a2078cd1bdd8.png) The **Settings**screen is displayed.
4. Click **Login as Admin**. ![](/images/8671144/3b700b8df0b1.png) The Login screen is displayed.
5. On the Login Page, tap **Login with Face**. ![](/images/8671144/9bb4b447e9f0.png) This will open the camera preview screen.
6. Tenant Admin and Supervisor needs to show their face. ![](/images/8671144/706f551b5f8f.png) As soon as face match is done, you are logged in to the Admin Mode.

## Log in Using SSO

### Pre-requisite:

- Tenant admin and Supervisor's Email ID needs to be updated in the Tenant Admin Portal.
- The endpoint must be paired. For further details, refer [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint)

### Steps to log in with SSO Options:

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect**![](/images/10288412/f7c4703dd471.png)**App**.**Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the preview screen of the App. For more, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint). ![](/images/8671144/bdb26aa81cf8.png)
2. Double tap with two fingers on the preview screen to launch the Admin Mode.
3. Tap **Yes- Continue.** ![](/images/8616263/a2078cd1bdd8.png) The **Settings**screen is displayed.
4. Click **Login as Admin**. ![](/images/8671144/3b700b8df0b1.png) The Login screen is displayed.
5. On the login page, tap **Login with SSO**. ![](/images/8671144/da38316161ba.png) The screen with **SSO options** is displayed. ![](/images/8671144/6eb10904266d.png)

**Note:** Oloid Connect uses **SSO login** and also allows you to log in to the portal with your [Google](/resources/integrations/sso/oloid-connect-first-time-login-using-google-sso) , [Microsoft](/resources/integrations/sso/oloid-connect-first-time-login-using-microsoft-sso), and [Okta](/resources/integrations/sso/oloid-connect-first-time-login-using-okta-sso).
