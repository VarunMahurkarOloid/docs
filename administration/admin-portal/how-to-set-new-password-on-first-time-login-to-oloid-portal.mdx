To access Oloid Portal, you should be a tenant admin. Once the tenant admin account is created, you will receive the URL and credentials (username and temporary password) for the portal on your registered email id.

Once you first login, the system prompts you to set your password.

To set new Password

1. Launch your browser.
2. Enter the URL provided for you in the browser’s address bar. Then, press **Enter** on your keyboard.
3. Enter your login information in the appropriate fields as provided in the email.![](/images/8423861/0bd5c218b1c0.png)
4. Click **LOGIN**.The **Set New Password** screen is displayed.![](/images/8423861/da0e64c931d5.png)
5. Enter New Password.**Note**: Password must contain at least one uppercase, one lowercase letter, one number, no consecutive or sequence characters The password should not contain any part of username.
6. Enter Confirm Password.
7. Click **LOGIN**.The Oloid Portal is displayed.
