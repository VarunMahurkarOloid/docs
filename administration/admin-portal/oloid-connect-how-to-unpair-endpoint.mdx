## Introduction

This document explains how to unpair an endpoint using the **Oloid Connect**application.

## Prerequisites

- The Oloid Connect endpoint must be paired. For more, refer to [Oloid Connect – How to Pair an Endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint)
- You must have Tenant Admin or Supervisor access.

## Steps to Unpair Endpoint

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect**![](/images/16159084/f7c4703dd471.png) **App**.**Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the preview screen of the App. For more, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint). ![](/images/16159084/bdb26aa81cf8.png)
2. Log in to Admin mode. For more refer to[Oloid Connect - How to Log in to Admin Mode](/administration/admin-portal/how-to-log-in-to-admin-mode-in-the-oloid-connect-app)The **Users** page is displayed.
3. Tap **Settings**. ![](/images/16159084/d97471e6f0c7.png) The **Settings** page is displayed.
4. Tap **Unpair**. ![](/images/16159084/8b70665b0953.png) The **Confirm Unpair** pop up box is displayed.
5. Tap **Unpair.** ![](/images/16159084/cf75fa5a443f.png) The Success pop up is displayed and Oloid Connect Endpoint is unpaired successfully. ![](/images/16159084/19c1ba9ac105.png) Keywords | Oloid Connect Unpair Endpoint | Unpair Oloid Connect Endpoint | Oloid Connect Endpoint|
