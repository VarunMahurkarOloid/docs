**Pre-requisite**

- Collect the images(faces) of the employees. The users can take pictures from their phone and send it to the Portal Admin to use in the portal.
- Create one folder per employee with images(faces). Each folder should be named as primary ID. A maximum of five images can be uploaded.
- To upload faces for multiple users, combine all individual user folders into a single folder and then upload the consolidated folder.

**Steps to onboard faces in bulk:**

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Directory** on the left panel menu. ![](/images/8185520/b9c707f0a9b1.png) The **Users** page is displayed.
3. Select **Import** > **Import** **Faces** from **Directory** menu panel. ![](/images/8185520/3a8530e37889.png) The **Import Faces** page is displayed. ![](/images/8185520/57e95d6eeb8d.png)
4. Click **Import Faces**. ![Image](/images/8185520/c4dd23d60e0d.png) The **Import Faces** pop-up box is displayed.
5. Drag and drop the images to upload. ![](/images/8185520/861f37d793ab.png)
6. Click **Import**.The images are uploaded and listed on **Import Faces** page.
