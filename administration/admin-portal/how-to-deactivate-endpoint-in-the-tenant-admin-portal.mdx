**Steps to Deactivate Endpoint:**

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Endpoints** on the left panel menu. ![](/images/8463355/4a8482e52907.png) The Endpoints page is displayed.
3. Select**Endpoint Name**to edit. ![](/images/8463355/fd907f380105.png) The Endpointdetail page is displayed.
4. Go to **Action**dropdown > select **Deactivate**. ![](/images/8463355/2ada21c90311.png) The **Confirm Deactivation** pop-up box is displayed. ![](/images/8463355/34e71dd8fb0d.png)
5. Click **Confirm**. The endpoint is deactivated and archived. ![](/images/8463355/9712af7dbb6f.png)
