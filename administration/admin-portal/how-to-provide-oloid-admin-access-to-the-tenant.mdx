To provide Oloid admin access, follow the below steps:

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the homepage of the portal, click **user profile icon**on the bottom left corner of the menu panel.![](/images/8811277/b47163bcb2e4.png)The user profile menu is displayed.
3. Select **Tenant** **Settings**. ![Image](/images/8811277/b5a63d6e40d4.png) The **Tenant Settings**page is displayed. ![](/images/8811277/be747d06bbff.png)
4. In **Details** tab, click **Support.** ![](/images/8811277/7388991b850e.png) Enable **Give OLOID admin access to the tenant**to OLOID admin to provide access to Oloid portal.Enter **Technical Support Email.**Enter **Customer Support Email.**Enter **Support URL.**Enter **Support Phone Number.**
5. Click **Save**. The setting is saved successfully.
