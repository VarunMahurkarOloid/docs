Steps to download users:

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Directory** on the left menu panel. ![Image](/images/11401401/6142e01fd801.png) The **Users** page is displayed.
3. Select **Export all users**![](/images/11401401/2d4af62f74cf.png) icon to download users from connection or group.![](/images/11401401/78cc46788a20.png)The**Export to Excel**pop-up box is displayed. ![](/images/11401401/2403423b8c4e.png)
4. Do the following:**Connection**: Select this option to export users associated with a specific connection.Select the desired connection from the **Connections** drop-down. ![](/images/11401401/132f611e45d7.png)The selected connection is added in the Connections field.**Group**: Export users associated with a specific group.Select the desired group from the **Groups** drop-down. ![](/images/11401401/47020a30dc4a.png)The selected group is added in the Groups field.
5. Click **Download**.
6. Click **Save**.The report file is downloaded to your local device. Video TutorialWatch the video [How to Download Users in Tenant Admin Portal-Video Tutorial](/administration/admin-portal/how-to-download-users-in-tenant-admin-portal-video-tutorial) ​
