## Introduction

The Oloid Verify app allows Tenant Admin to assign a location to an endpoint during the setup process. Adding a location ensures accurate tracking and management of endpoints within your organization.

## Prerequisite

- You must have a Tenant Admin or Supervisor account.
- You must download the Oloid Verify app. To download refer [How to download Oloid Verify App](/passwordless/verify/oloid-verify-how-to-download-and-install-the-app).

## Steps to add location to an endpoint

1. Launch the **Oloid Verify** app. ![](/images/10198654/65448eab37c7.png)
2. Log in to Admin mode in Oloid Verify app. To login, refer [How to log in to the admin mode of Oloid Verify App as an Admin or Supervisor.](/administration/admin-portal/oloid-verify-how-to-log-in-to-admin-mode-as-an-admin-or-supervisor)The location access pop-up box is displayed.
3. On the pop-up box, tap an option:**Allow Once:** Each time you open the app, it will request access your location.**Allow While Using App:** The app can access your location only when you are actively using it.**Don't Allow:** The app cannot access your location, even while you are using it.**Note:** You can also update location from the device settings. To update refer **[How to update location in Oloid Verify.](/administration/admin-portal/oloid-verify-how-to-update-location-permission-on-ipad)** ![](/images/8601170/e874474d54df.png) **Note:** You can now log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal** to check the location that is added to the specific endpoint.
