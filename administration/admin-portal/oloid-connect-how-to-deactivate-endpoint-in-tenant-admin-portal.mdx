## Introduction

An Oloid Connect endpoint that is already paired and in use can be deactivated when it is no longer required for active use.

**Note:** Once you pair an endpoint with Oloid Connect application, it gets saved and appears in the Tenant Admin Portal.

## Prerequisites

- You must have Admin access to the Tenant Admin Portal.
- The Oloid Connect endpoint must be paired and available in the Tenant Admin Portal. For more, refer to[Oloid Connect – How to Pair an Endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint)

## Steps to Deactivate Endpoint

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Endpoints** on the left menu panel. ![](/images/10511068/c63f6b503e62.png) The **Endpoints** page is displayed.
3. Select the Oloid Connect Endpoint. ![](/images/16096126/13925ed55a83.png) The **Endpoint Details**page is displayed. ![](/images/16096126/639bd6c58a20.png)
4. Do the following to deactivate the endpoint:Click **Action**.From the drop-down menu, click **Deactivate**. ![](/images/16096126/4336096d3b4f.png) The **Confirm Deactivation** Pop up box is displayed.
5. Click **Confirm**. ![](/images/16096126/7fd0c4e1b6ee.png) The Oloid Connect Endpoint is deactivated successfully. ![](/images/16096126/55e8907e2035.png) Keywords|Deactivate Endpoint |Oloid Connect Endpoint| Deactivate Oloid Connect Endpoint|
