## Introduction

This document explains how to update the location permission for the Oloid Verify app on iPad.  
​

## Prerequisite

You must have the Oloid Verify app installed on the iPad. For more, refer to   [How to Download and Install the Oloid Verify App](/passwordless/verify/oloid-verify-how-to-download-and-install-the-app)

## Steps to update location permission

1. Go to **Settings** in **iPad**. ![Image](/images/10204617/c64967cca550.png) The Settings menu is displayed.
2. Search **Oloid Verify App** and open. ![](/images/10204617/c0f25d0eb077.png) The Oloid Verify app menu is displayed.
3. Tap **Location**. ![](/images/10204617/158056f552d6.png) The **Location access**menu is displayed. ![](/images/10204617/8a8d5d460d11.png)
4. Select **an option.****Never:** The app cannot use your location, even when you’re using the app.**Ask Next Time Or When I Share**: Every time you open the app, it’ll ask to use your location.**While Using the App:**The app can use your location only when you're using that app.
5. Enable **Precise Location** to allow the app to detect your specific location.**Note:** Precise Location option appears when you allow location access.The location is updated successfully.
