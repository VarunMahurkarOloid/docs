## Introduction

**HR Picture Verification** enables users to authenticate by matching their live image with the HR image stored in the system. Depending on whether a face credential already exists and the user’s consent status, the system guides users through authentication or enrollment to complete the login process.

## ​Prerequisites

- You have the **login URL** provided to access the User portal.
- HR Picture Verification must be enabled in the Tenant settings
- Your **Employee ID or Email ID** must be registered in the system
- Camera access must be available on your device (if required)

## Steps to log into User Portal

1. Launch your browser.
2. Enter the provided **URL** in the browser’s address bar and press **Enter**.The **Oloid Login**page is displayed.
3. Click **Verify.****Note**: You will only see the authentication methods (Face, Badge and NFC/ID) enabled for you. Button names may vary based on configuration. Contact [Support](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) if no authentication method appears. ![](/images/14815240/f27f4553bca1.png) The User Authentication screen is displayed.
4. Enter **Employee ID/ Email ID**.
5. Click **Continue.** ![](/images/14815240/2f85d5c2af41.png) Based on the configurations, one of the following scenarios will occur[Face Credential Already Exists](#h_cfa2aa6e2e)[Face Credential Not Available](#h_4a334f63f0)

## Face Credential Already Exists

If your face is already registered in the system:

1. **Face already enrolled** error popup is displayed Click **Okay.**The Authentication screen is displayed ![](/images/14815240/c8b800c5bc11.png) The **Oloid Login**page is displayed.
2. Click **Login with Face.**Refer to: **[How to login with face in Oloid User Portal](/administration/user-portal/how-to-login-with-face-in-oloid-user-portal)**User is successfully logged in.

## Face Credential Not Available

If your face credential is not registered there can be the following scenarios:

- **[Consent Provided](#h_4589c16a3c)**
- **[Consent Not Provided](#h_e77e5f5b04)**
- **[Re-consent pending](#h_ecda92e680)**

### Consent Provided

The camera opens.

  Provide camera permission if prompted.

Position your face in front of the camera and tap the Capture button to authenticate face.**Note**: If you do not tap the Capture button, the app automatically captures your face after a few seconds. ![](/images/14815240/885ebc0939a1.png) Once the image is captured, it is matched against the HR Verification picture in the oloid databaseYou are signed in to the Oloid User Portal Successfully.

### Consent Not Provided

1. The Consent page is displayed.
2. Click **Agree** to submit consent. ![](/images/14815240/349bc8561e0f.png) The consent is submitted successfully. The camera opens. ![](/images/14815240/efc4cb41f8a8.png)
3. Position your face in front of the camera and tap the Capture button to authenticate face.**Note**: If you do not tap the Capture button, the app automatically captures your face after a few seconds. ![](/images/14815240/885ebc0939a1.png) You are signed in to the Oloid User Portal Successfully.

### Re-consent pending

The re-submit consent screen appears

1. Click **Agree.****Note:** You need to provide your consent to proceed to next steps if consent is enabled for you. ![](/images/14815240/38ae0adbada2.png) The camera opens. ![](/images/14815240/efc4cb41f8a8.png)
2. Position your face in front of the camera and tap the Capture button to authenticate face.**Note**: If you do not tap the Capture button, the app automatically captures your face after a few seconds. ![](/images/14815240/885ebc0939a1.png) You are signed in to the Oloid User Portal Successfully.
