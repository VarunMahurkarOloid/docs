Steps to add face:

1. Login to the **Oloid User Portal**.
2. On the homepage, click**Face**. ![Image](/images/9874419/f15168b91b68.png) The **Face Credential**page is displayed.**Note**: You need to provide your consent to proceed to next steps if consent is enabled for you. ![](/images/9874419/e9c91852d17a.png) Select **Add Face**. ![](/images/9874419/cc5b35842f01.png) **Note**: Provide camera permission if prompted. ![](/images/8472935/efc4cb41f8a8.png) This opens the camera on your device.Look at the camera to confirm your identity. ![](/images/8472935/885ebc0939a1.png) The pop-up screen appears with the captured image. ![](/images/9874419/75bb08192d28.png) Do one of the following:Select **Add Face** to submit the image.The face is submitted successfully and is assigned as a face credential.Select **Retake** to take your photo again.The camera turns on to retake the photo.**Note**: You can add a maximum of five faces per user.Your face should be visible in the camera, or else it might get rejected. It is recommended to avoid wearing things that cover your facial features such as sunglasses, masks, headbands, etc.
3. The face is added successfully. ![](/images/9874419/be101dfb7d03.png)
