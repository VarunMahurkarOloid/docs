To replace and assign NFC:

1. Log in to the **Oloid User Portal**.
2. On the credential page of the Oloid User Portal, select **NFC**. ![](/images/9459621/7a3db72f73ee.png) The **NFC Credential** page is displayed.
3. Select **Replace NFC**. ![](/images/9459621/c5c9c2c0ae65.png)
4. On the confirmation pop-up box do the following:Select **Report lost NFC Tag**. (Optional)Select **NFC Tag not working**. (Optional) Select **Replace NFC**. ![](/images/9459621/c64e9e2d7fb8.png) The confirmation pop-up to ensure that the NFC Tag reader is connected to your device is displayed.
5. Click**Okay**.![](/images/9459621/536cb4d8a5d7.png) The **Scan your NFC Tag**page is displayed. ![](/images/9459621/a2f00d89646e.png)
6. Do any of the following:Enter the NFC number.Click on the**Scan or Type Data**textbox area. Tap the **NFC card** on the **Card Reader** to scan it.**Note:**If you do not click on the Scan or Type Data textbox area, you will not see the data in the text field.Click **Submit**.You successfully assigned a new NFC credential. ![](/images/9459621/48c7b0575615.png) Video TutorialWatch the video [How to replace and assign NFC using Oloid User Portal- Video Tutorial](/administration/user-portal/how-to-replace-and-assign-nfc-using-oloid-user-portal-video-tutorial)
