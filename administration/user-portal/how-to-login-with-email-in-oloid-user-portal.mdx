**Pre-requisite**: You should have a Oloid account.

Steps to Log in with Email:

1. Launch your browser.
2. Enter the URL provided for you in the browser’s address bar. Then, press **Enter** on your keyboard.The Oloid User Portal login page is displayed. ![Image](/images/11324573/ca646a3ca4b2.png)
3. Select **Login with Email**.The **Login with Email** page is displayed. ![](/images/11324573/3f667b3e1bf6.png)
4. Do the following:Enter your email address in the **Email ID** field.Click **Get Verification Code**.A verification code is sent to your registered email ID.Enter the Verification code.**Note:**The One Time Verification Code expires after 60 seconds. ![](/images/11324573/efa8120662ad.png) Click **Login**.The Oloid User Portal is displayed.**Note**: Oloid uses **SSO login** and also allows you to log in to the User Portal with your Google, Microsoft, Okta account or your configured Login options ( Face, badge etc).
