To enroll NFC:

1. Log in to the **Oloid User Portal**.
2. On the credential page of the Oloid User Portal, select **NFC**. ![](/images/9459595/8118541e16e4.png) The **NFC Credential** page is displayed.
3. Select **Enroll for NFC Login**. ![](/images/9459595/568899bad212.png) The **Scan your NFC Tag**page is displayed. ![](/images/9459595/eaa510d5988a.png) The confirmation pop-up to ensure that the NFC Tag reader is connected to your device is displayed.
4. Click **Okay**.![](/images/9459595/536cb4d8a5d7.png)
5. Do any of the following:Enter the NFC number.Click on the**Scan or Type Data**textbox area. Tap the **NFC card** on the **Card Reader** to scan it.**Note:**If you do not click on the Scan or Type Data textbox area, you will not see the data in the text field.Click **Submit**. You are successfully assigned an NFC credential. ![](/images/9459595/3415c12f84a4.png) Video TutorialWatch the video [How to enroll for NFC Login using Oloid User Portal- Video Tutorial](/administration/user-portal/how-to-enroll-for-nfc-login-using-oloid-user-portal-video-tutorial)
