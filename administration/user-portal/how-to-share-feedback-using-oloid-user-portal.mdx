Steps to share feedback:

1. Login to the **Oloid User Portal**.
2. On the homepage, click menu panel ![](/images/9874142/f19cdbadec5b.png) icon on the top right corner.
3. Select**Profile**. ![Image](/images/9874142/0a8f72b982d2.png) The **Your Profile** page is displayed.
4. Click **Share app feedback**. ![Image](/images/9874142/08b7eee53c18.png) The **Share app feedback** page is displayed.
5. Do the following:Enter feedback in **Type here** field.Click **Submit**. ![](/images/9874142/01824f718570.png) The feedback is sent successfully. ![](/images/9874142/95e2aa23c845.png)
