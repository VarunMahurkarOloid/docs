### Introduction

This document provides step-by-step instructions to replace and assign a badge in the Oloid User Portal.

### Steps to replace and assign Badge:

1. Log in to the the **Oloid User Portal**.
2. On the credential page of the Oloid User Portal, select **Badge**. ![](/images/9464983/11eeb681f9f1.png) The **Badge Credential** page is displayed.
3. Select **Replace Badge**. ![](/images/9464983/9d10c80d0a48.png)
4. On the confirmation pop-up box do the following:Select **Report Lost Badge**.(Optional)Select **Badge not Working**.(Optional)Select **Replace Badge**. ![](/images/9464983/a38cd1238ab2.png) The confirmation to ensure that the Badge reader is connect to your device is displayed.
5. Click **Okay**. ![](/images/9464983/ce62d593da30.png) The **Scan your Badge**page is displayed. ![](/images/9459048/a3c0544e6a44.png)
6. Do any of the following:Enter the badge card number.Click on the**Scan or Type Data**textbox area. Tap the **Badge** on the **Badge Reader** to scan it.**Note:**If you do not click on the Scan or Type Data textbox area, you will not see the data in the text field.
7. Click **Submit**.You are successfully assigned a new badge on badge authentication, and a success message is displayed. ![](/images/9464983/aec7c3c2fedf.png) The badge is replaced and displayed under the Badge Credential. ![](/images/9464983/c785674f215b.png) Video TutorialWatch the video [How to replace and assign Badge using Oloid User Portal- Video Tutorial](/administration/user-portal/how-to-replace-and-assign-badge-using-oloid-user-portal-video-tutorial)
