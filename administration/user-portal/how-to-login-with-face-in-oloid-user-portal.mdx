steps to login with face:

1. Go to the **Oloid User Portal**.The **login page** is displayed.
2. Click **Login with Face**. ![](/images/10162035/0ca385a4f433.png) The **Login with Face** screen is displayed.
3. Enter**Primary ID** > click **Next**. ![](/images/10162035/c103344373c8.png) The camera opens to authenticate the face.
4. Place your face in front of the camera for facial authentication and press **Enter or Spacebar.** ![](/images/10162035/8ccedfea3c01.png) The **Processing** starts for the face taken. ![](/images/10162035/77201cca929e.png) You are signed in to the Oloid User Portal Successfully. ![](/images/10162035/05a74eafbe19.png)
