Steps to view QR code:

1. Log in to the **Oloid User Portal**.
2. On the homepage, click **QR Code**. ![Image](/images/9901402/fe73ad0fbcdc.png) The **Assign QR Code** page is displayed and you can view the QR Code on the page. ![](/images/9901402/a201334b4f1a.png) **Note:** You can also download the QR Code.
