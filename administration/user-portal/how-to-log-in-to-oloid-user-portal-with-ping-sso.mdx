To enable **Ping SSO Login** on Oloid User Portal, you need to raise a ticket with [Oloid Support.](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) Your Ping SSO Login will be enabled within 48 hrs.

**Pre-requisite**

- You should be a user in the Tenant Admin Portal.
- You must enable SSO options in Tenant Admin Portal. For more details, refer to [How to configure Oloid User Portal in Tenant Admin Portal](/administration/user-portal/oloid-user-portal-how-to-configure-oloid-user-portal-in-tenant-admin-portal)

**Steps to log in to Oloid User Portal using Ping SSO Login option:**

1. Launch your browser.
2. Enter the URL provided for you in the browser’s address bar. Then, press **Enter** on your keyboard.The **Oloid User Portal** page is displayed.
3. Click **Log in with SSO**. ![](/images/11122318/763652e87727.png) The configured Login options are displayed.
4. Click **Continue with Ping**. ![](/images/9556317/595c4d635d58.png) The Sign in pop-up box is displayed.
5. Do the following.Enter your**Email address**.Enter your **Password**.
6. Click **Sign on**. ![](/images/9556317/5d4b107007ee.png) You are logged in to the Oloid User Portal using Ping SSO successfully. ![](/images/9556317/af4829b8c965.png) ​ ​
