Steps to create a Flow:

1. Launch your chrome browser.
2. Enter **localhost: 3000**in your browser address bar. Then, press Enter on your keyboard.The Workflow Login page is displayed.![](/images/8574780/5d0ee07e4c85.png)
3. Enter username and password in the appropriate fields.
4. Click **Login**.The Oloid Workflow application page is displayed.![](/images/8574780/a4d4932ed68b.png)
5. Click **New Flow** on the top menu.![](/images/8574780/b16485032cab.png) The **New Flow** pop-up box is displayed.![](/images/8574780/bf3cc0375915.png)
6. Do the following in the pop-up box:Enter name of the flow in the **Create Flow** textbox.Enter **Description**.
7. Click **Save**.The new blank flow is created and added as a tab in the application![](/images/8574780/dacfc70eceb9.png)
8. Drag and drop the elements from the Node Panel to create your desired flow.![](/images/8574780/83be08e4f6b7.png)
9. Click **Deploy.**The flow is successfully deployed.
