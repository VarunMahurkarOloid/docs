## Prerequisite:

You must download the Oloid Connect app from App Store. For more details, refer [How to download Oloid Connect app](/passwordless/ios/connect/how-to-download-and-install-the-oloid-connect-app-in-ipad).

## Steps to log in with Microsoft SSO:

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect**![](/images/10288412/f7c4703dd471.png) app. ![](/images/10293864/81ba504b16bd.png)
2. Tap **Pair as Supervisor.** ![](/images/10293864/8a9b62c1a00e.png) The Welcome screen is displayed.
3. Enter **Tenant Name,**then tap **Next**. ![](/images/10293864/5221e300bb3a.png) The**Login** page is displayed.
4. Tap **Login with SSO**. ![](/images/10293864/3ea7c41ad72d.png) The screen with **SSO options** is displayed.
5. Select **Continue with Microsoft**. ![](/images/10293864/6fef7708bf59.png) The confirmation pop-up is displayed.
6. Click **Continue**. ![](/images/10293864/673b5530b377.png) The **Sign in** page is displayed.
7. Do the following:Enter **Email ID**.Click **Next**. ![](/images/10293864/d725327fd3c2.png) The **Enter password** screen is displayed.
8. Do the following:Enter **Password**.Click **Sign in**. ![](/images/10293864/b12dd68c6f76.png) You are successfully logged in to Oloid Connect Admin Mode.
