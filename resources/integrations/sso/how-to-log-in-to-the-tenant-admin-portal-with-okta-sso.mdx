**Prerequisite:**

- You should be a user in the Tenant Admin Portal with **Admin/Supervisor** role.

**Steps to log in with Okta SSO:**

1. Launch your browser.
2. Enter the URL provided for you in the browser’s address bar > press **Enter** on your keyboard. The Tenant Admin Portal **Login**page is displayed.
3. Click **Log in with SSO.** ![](/images/11744118/8be0bc89f301.png) The **Configured login options(s)** page is displayed.
4. Click **Continue with Okta**to log in to Tenant Admin Portal with Okta SSO. ![](/images/11744118/c0d608bed400.png) The Sign in pop-up box is displayed.
5. On the pop-up box, do the following:Enter **Username**.Enter **Password**.Select **Remember me** to stay signed in on this device without having to re-enter your credentials each time you log in. (optional)Click **Sign In**. ![](/images/11744118/699d0779d5d8.png) You are logged in to the Tenant Admin Portal using Okta SSO successfully. ![](/images/11744118/aacdd8e0e9c6.png)
