## Prerequisite:

You must download the Oloid Connect from App Store. For more details, refer [How to download Oloid Connect app](/passwordless/ios/connect/how-to-download-and-install-the-oloid-connect-app-in-ipad).

## Steps to log in with Google SSO:

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect**![](/images/10288412/f7c4703dd471.png) app. ![](/images/10293811/81ba504b16bd.png)
2. Tap **Pair as Supervisor.** ![](/images/10293811/8a9b62c1a00e.png) The Welcome screen is displayed.
3. Enter **Tenant Name**, then tap **Next**. ![](/images/10293811/5221e300bb3a.png) TheLogin page is displayed.
4. Tap **Login with SSO**. ![](/images/10293811/3ea7c41ad72d.png) The screen with **SSO options** is displayed.
5. Select **Continue with Google**. ![](/images/10293811/22b3164fe458.png) The confirmation pop-up is displayed.
6. Click **Continue**. ![](/images/10293811/9e78dd233622.png) The **Sign in** screen is displayed.
7. Do the following:Enter **Email ID**.Click **Next**. ![](/images/10293811/2ebc5030fb7e.png) The **Welcome** screen is displayed.
8. Do the following:Enter **Password**.Click **Next**. ![](/images/10293811/7465d345b9cd.png) You are logged in to admin mode in Oloid Connect successfully. ![](/images/10293811/9dd0d4269acd.png)
