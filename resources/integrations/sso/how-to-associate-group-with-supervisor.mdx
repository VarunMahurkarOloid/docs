Steps to associate group with Supervisor:

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the homepage of the portal, click **user profile icon**on the bottom left corner of the menu panel.![](/images/10299914/c8560f399688.png)The user profile menu is displayed.
3. Click **Settings**.![](/images/8836666/16487879eebb.png)The **Tenant Settings** page is displayed.
4. Go to **Admin Controls** > **Supervisors**.![](/images/8836666/71dbff7fb537.png)
5. Click **kebab menu** (three vertical dots) and select **Associate with Group**for a supervisor.![](/images/8836666/21021402152e.png)The **Confirm Group Association**pop-up box is displayed.![](/images/8836666/609bd8d9aed4.png)
6. Click **Confirm**.Now, the supervisor can view the users associated with the group and perform required actions on the assigned endpoints.
