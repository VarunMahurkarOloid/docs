To enable **PingOne SSO Login** on Oloid Portal, you need to raise a ticket with [Oloid Support.](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) Your PingOne SSO Login will be enabled within 48 hrs.

​**Pre-requisite**

- You should be a user in the Oloid Portal with **Admin/Supervisor** role.

Follow the below steps to log into Oloid Portal using **PingOne SSO Login** option:

1. Launch your browser.
2. Enter the URL provided for you in the browser’s address bar > press **Enter** on your keyboard.The Oloid LOGIN page is displayed.
3. Click **Log in with SSO.** ![](/images/11136469/8be0bc89f301.png) The configured Log in options are displayed.
4. Click **Continue with Ping**to log in to Oloid Platform with PingOne SSO. ![](/images/11136469/956d6c492426.png)The Sign in pop-up box is displayed.
5. Enter your **Ping User Name** > enter **Password**> click **Sign On**.

You are logged in to the Oloid Portal using PingOne SSO successfully.
