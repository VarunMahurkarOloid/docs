_Last Updated: Dec 2024_

This document provides a list of Oloid’s current sub-processors, including their location, purpose, and links to additional information. Oloid may update this list periodically as services evolve.

## Infrastructure Sub-Processors

| **Sub-Processor** | **Location of Processing** | **Purpose** | **More Information** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amazon Web Services (AWS) | USA | Cloud hosting, data storage, and compute services (e.g., Cognito for identity management, DynamoDB for database, S3 for encrypted storage, ECS for container orchestration) | [https://aws.amazon.com/](https://aws.amazon.com) |
| Google Cloud Platform (GCP) | USA | Cloud hosting and related services (e.g., Firebase for app backend) | [https://cloud.google.com/](https://cloud.google.com) |

## Platform and Application Sub-Processors

| **Sub-Processor** | **Location of Processing** | **Purpose** | **More Information** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Twilio | USA | Communication (SMS, voice) | [https://www.twilio.com/](https://www.twilio.com) |
| Vanta | USA | Security monitoring and compliance | [https://vanta.com/](https://vanta.com) |
| Intercom | USA | Customer support and in-app messaging | [https://www.intercom.com/](https://www.intercom.com) |
| Boost Security | USA | Code scanning and security analysis | [https://www.boostsecurity.io](https://www.boostsecurity.io) |

## Operations and Business Support Sub-Processors

| **Sub-Processor** | **Location of Processing** | **Purpose** | **More Information** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bill.com | USA | Financial operations (billing, invoicing) | [https://www.bill.com/](https://www.bill.com) |
| DocuSign | USA | Electronic signatures, contract management | [https://www.docusign.com/](https://www.docusign.com) |
| Google Workspace | USA | Productivity (email, docs, storage) | [https://workspace.google.com/](https://workspace.google.com) |
| Slack | USA | Internal team communication | [https://slack.com/](https://slack.com) |
| Zoom | USA | Video conferencing and webinars | [https://zoom.us/](https://zoom.us) |
