## Prerequisite:

You must download the Oloid Connect app from App Store. For more details, refer [How to download Oloid Connect app](/passwordless/ios/connect/how-to-download-and-install-the-oloid-connect-app-in-ipad).

## Steps to log in with Okta SSO:

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect**![](/images/10288412/f7c4703dd471.png) app. ![](/images/10345413/81ba504b16bd.png)
2. Tap **Pair as Supervisor.** ![](/images/10293811/8a9b62c1a00e.png) The **Welcome** page is displayed.
3. Enter **Tenant Name,**then tap **Next**. ![](/images/10345413/ad89cb4d5d7a.png) The**Login** page is displayed.
4. Tap **Login with SSO**. ![](/images/10345413/3ea7c41ad72d.png) The screen with **SSO options** is displayed.
5. Select **Continue with Okta**. ![](/images/10345413/73c54e7991c0.png) The confirmation pop-up is displayed.
6. Tap **Continue**. ![](/images/10345413/88380e0bed23.png) The Sign In screen is displayed.
7. Enter **Username**> tap **Next**. ![](/images/10345413/b1e6e0ffa4ad.png)
8. Enter **Password** in the **Password** field > tap **Sign In**. ![](/images/10345413/0769ca7d69d9.png) You are logged in to Admin Mode successfully.
