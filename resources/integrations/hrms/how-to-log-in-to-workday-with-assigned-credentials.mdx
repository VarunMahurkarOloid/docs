## Introduction

This document provides step-by-step instructions to log in to Workday using a Badge credential through Oloid authentication.

## Prerequisite

A credential must be assigned to the user in the Tenant Admin Portal.

**Assumption:** Badge is assigned to the user.

## Steps to Log in to Workday with Badge

1. Launch the Workday.The **Workday Sign In** screen is displayed.
2. Click **Oloid**. ![](/images/15030169/47b5f5abe1ba.png) The **Welcome**screen is displayed.
3. Click **Login with** **Badge**.**Note:** Based on the configured authentication methods, users may also authenticate using Face or NFC credentials. ![](/images/15030169/90ee0c8bfe9b.png) The **Scan Badge at the Reader**pop-up box is displayed.
4. Tap the Badge on the Badge Reader to scan it. ![](/images/15030169/c81d1676b8e6.png) After successful authentication, workday home screen is displayed. ​![](/images/15030169/659d1f31a7ec.png)
