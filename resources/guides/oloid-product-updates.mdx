## Oloid Connect

## RELEASE UPDATE - AUGUST 2025

### What's New

- **Face as Second Factor**Users can now use face authentication as an additional security factor, enhancing both convenience and protection. ​
- **Liveliness Check for Second Factor**A liveliness check has been integrated into the face authentication process when used as the second factor. This ensures real-time identity verification and strengthens security by preventing spoofing or presentation attacks.

## Oloid Passwordless

## December Updates - 2024

**Introducing Version 1.0.13**

- **Self-Enrollment Made Easy** With our new self-enrollment feature, users can effortlessly enroll without needing assistance from tenant admins or supervisors.
- **Presence Detection for Enhanced Security** Automatically lock your desktop when you step away, send supervisor alerts, and protects against unauthorized devices for added protection.

---

## November Updates - 2024

**Introducing Version 1.1.27**

- **Auto-Unblock for a Hassle-Free Experience** Automatically unlocks your screen after a successful login using Windows credentials.
- **Improved Blocked Screen Messaging** Updated messages now include a timer for Auto-Unblock, keeping you informed at a glance.
