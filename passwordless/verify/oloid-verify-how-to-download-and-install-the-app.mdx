## Introduction

The **Oloid Verify App** allows secure and contactless authentication for workforce management and access control. To begin using the app on your iPad, you must download and install the app from the App Store.

## Prerequisites

#### Before You Begin

- A stable and reliable internet connection is available.
- You have a valid Apple Account (Apple ID).If you do not already have an Apple Account, follow Apple's official guide to create one: [How to Create a New Apple Account (Apple Support)](https://support.apple.com/en-in/108647?device-type=iphone&utm_source=chatgpt.com).
- Verify that your device meets the minimum hardware and software requirements. For more details, refer [Oloid Verify - Hardware and Software Requirements.](/passwordless/verify/oloid-verify-hardware-and-software-requirements)

## Steps to download and install the Oloid Verify app

1. Locate the **App Store** on your home screen and tap on it to launch the app. ![](/images/8592228/34cf7013faa3.png)
2. Tap on the **Search** icon located at the bottom right corner of the screen. ![](/images/8592228/54f49eeb4ba0.png) The Search bar is displayed.
3. In the search bar at the top of the screen, type **Oloid Verify** and tap on the search result. ![](/images/8592228/88e32a8fc89d.png)
4. Select **Oloid Verify** app.**Note:** If you are downloading the app for the first time from the App Store, you will see the **Get** button; otherwise, the **download** icon will appear. ![](/images/8592228/fd638d4825b5.png)
5. Tap the **Get**button located next to the **Oloid Verif**y app icon. ![](/images/8592228/5bf22842cace.png) **Note**: If prompted, enter your Apple ID password or use Touch ID/Face ID to confirm the download. Once the installation is complete, the **Get** button is changed to **Open** button.
6. Tap on the **Open** button to launch the **Oloid Verify** app. ![](/images/8592228/2714e4be3a37.png) The login screen is displayed. ![](/images/8592228/6fb6685413f6.png)
