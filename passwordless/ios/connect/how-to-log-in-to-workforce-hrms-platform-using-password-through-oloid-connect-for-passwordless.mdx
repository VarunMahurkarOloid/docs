**Pre-requisite**

- User must have an Okta account.

- Okta credential (user email ID) must be added to the user details page in Oloid Portal.

To log in with password:

1. Launch the Oloid Connect app on your iPad.
2. On the home page of the app, select **Login with Password**. ![](/images/9895757/e834c023d33b.png)
3. Enter your login credential in the respective fields.. ![](/images/9895757/9c3c086fcc8f.png) You are successfully signed in to the Okta system. ![](/images/9895757/e1d30618319e.png) Watch our video [How to login with Password through Oloid Connect for Passwordless](/passwordless/ios/connect/how-to-log-in-to-workforce-hrms-platform-using-password-through-oloid-connect-for-passwordless)
