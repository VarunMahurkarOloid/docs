**Pre-requisite**

- User must have an Okta account.
- Okta credential (user email ID) must be added to the user details page in Oloid Portal.
- The user face must be added in the Oloid Portal.

To log in with face:

1. Launch the Oloid Connect app on your iPad.
2. On the home page of the app, select **Face Auth**. ![](/images/9920262/9ef74a77bccf.png) This opens the camera on your device.
3. Look at the camera to confirm your identity. ![](/images/9920262/21c721708029.png) You are successfully signed in to the Okta system. ![](/images/9895757/e1d30618319e.png)
