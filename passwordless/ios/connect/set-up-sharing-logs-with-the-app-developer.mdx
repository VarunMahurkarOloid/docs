On the Oloid Verify/Connect app, you can share logs with the app developer in two ways:

[Upload logs to the app developer](#h_f8e2df270f)

[View and share logs with the app developer](#h_99ed227fe7)

## Pre-requisite

Endpoint configuration for debugLog should be done in the Oloid portal.

## Upload logs to the app developer

Steps to upload logs:

1. Launch the **Oloid Verify** app in preview mode.
2. Double-tap the screen with two fingers to log into **Admin mode**. ![](/images/8247618/59fb0417dd94.png)
3. Tap **YES-CONTINUE**. ![](/images/8247618/284f22bec597.png)**Settings** screen is displayed.
4. Tap on **Start** button in the **Health Check**section. ![](/images/8247618/7819fcc9f91c.png) The **Please Select Health Type** pop-up box is displayed.
5. Select **Upload Logs**.![](/images/8247618/8abf9af11844.png) The logs are successfully uploaded. View and share logs with the app developerSteps to view and share logs:

1. Launch the **Oloid Verify** app in preview mode.
2. Tap the screen four times using a single finger .![](/images/8247618/59fb0417dd94.png)The **Ondevice Sync Report** pop-up box is displayed.
3. Tap **VIEW LOGS**.![](/images/8247618/f61113f3ffca.png) The log report is displayed on the screen. ![](/images/8247618/09c9b78c8e32.png)
4. On the top right corner of the report page, you can do one of the following:Tap copy icon, to copy the log report to your file.Tap the share button to send the log report to the developer. ![](/images/8247618/425ff0908e42.png)
