## Introduction

To check hardware compatibility using the Oloid Connect app, you can perform a hardware check through Admin Mode to ensure the device meets the required specifications for proper functionality.

## Prerequisite

- The user must be a Tenant Admin or Supervisor.
- The endpoint must be paired.For more, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint on iPhone](/passwordless/ios/connect/how-to-pair-oloid-connect-endpoint-on-iphone)

## Steps to Check Hardware Compatibility

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect App**.**Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the initial screen of the App. ![](/images/13427870/f6d8940ab258.png)
2. Double tap with two fingers on the initial screen to launch the Admin Mode.The Admin mode pop up box is displayed.
3. Tap **Yes- Continue.** ![](/images/13427870/215b96454c59.png) The **Settings**screen is displayed.
4. Tap **Start** in the **Hardware Check** section. ![](/images/13427870/213c13139594.png) The pop-up to **select Hardware Type** is displayed.
5. Select **Endpoint**. ![](/images/13427870/b9c6f035ace6.png) The **Hardware Check** screen is displayed to check the details. ![](/images/13427870/4c6ee2c1aac7.png) Keywords|Oloid Connect iPhone hardware compatibility |Check hardware compatibility in Oloid Connect iPhone |Oloid Connect hardware check iPhone|
