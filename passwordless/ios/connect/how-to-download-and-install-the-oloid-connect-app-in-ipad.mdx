**Prerequisite:**

Make sure you have a stable internet connection and an Apple ID.

## Steps to download and install Oloid Connect App in iPad:

1. Locate the **App Store** on your home screen and tap on it to launch the application. ![](/images/8592076/34cf7013faa3.png)
2. Tap on the **Search** icon located at the upper right corner of the screen. ![](/images/8592076/98ee08f2fb07.png) The Search bar is displayed.
3. In the search bar at the top of the screen, enter **Oloid Connect** and tap on the search result. ![](/images/8592076/c7175bbd1c19.png)
4. Select **Oloid Connect**![](/images/10288412/f7c4703dd471.png) app. ![](/images/8592076/025f71d8445d.png)
5. Review the app's details, ratings, and user reviews to make sure it's the correct app you want to download. ![](/images/8592076/3aaa1d566f36.png)
6. Tap the **Get**button located next to the **Oloid Connect**![](/images/10288412/f7c4703dd471.png) app icon. ![](/images/8592076/1d74917e4a6b.png) **Note**: If prompted, enter your Apple ID password or use Touch ID/Face ID to confirm the download.Once the installation is complete, the **Get** button is changed to an **Open** button. ![](/images/8592076/ffce77df0592.png)
7. Tap the **Open** button to launch the **Oloid Connect**![](/images/10288412/f7c4703dd471.png) app. The welcome screen is displayed. ![](/images/8592076/81ba504b16bd.png)
