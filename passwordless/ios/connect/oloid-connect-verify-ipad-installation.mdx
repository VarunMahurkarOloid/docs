It is recommended to wall mount the iPad and also use an iPad Case/Cover.

Mount the iPad keeping the following in mind.

- Mount Height: The iPad(s) should be mounted at different heights so that users of all heights can verify their faces using the iPad.

- Power Source: The iPad must be mounted such that it can always be plugged into a power source.

- Lighting: Ensure proper lighting so that well-lit images may be captured.

- Sturdy Mount: The iPad must be mounted on a sturdy platform so that steady images are captured.
