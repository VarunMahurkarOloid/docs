Oloid Connect for Passwordless is a secure and convenient authentication solution that allows you to log in to your Workforce or HRMS platforms without the need for traditional passwords. By leveraging modern authentication methods such as Face Recognition, Badge, PIN, QR Code, or NFC, Oloid Connect offers a seamless and flexible login experience tailored to your device's configuration.

**Key Features:**

- **Self-Enrollment for Face Recognition**: Users can easily enroll their facial biometrics, ensuring quick and secure access without the hassle of remembering passwords.
- **Single-Factor Authentication**: Choose from various single-factor authenticators like Face, Badge, PIN, NFC/ID, or QR Code, depending on your preference.
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**: Enhance security by combining multiple authenticators. For instance:**NFC + PIN**: Requires both NFC-enabled device access and a PIN for added security.**QR Code + PIN**: Scan a QR code and enter a PIN for a secure, two-step verification process.**Face + PIN**: Combine facial recognition with a PIN for heightened security.**Badge + NFC**: Use your badge in combination with an NFC-enabled device for secure authentication.**QR Code + Badge**: Scan a QR code and authenticate with your badge for enhanced security.
- **Customizable MFA Settings**: You can activate Multi-Factor Authentication at the application level and customize it to meet your organization's security requirements.

With seamless integration, Oloid Connect for Passwordless, enables frictionless  access to enterprise applications upon successful authentication. The intuitive interface available on iPads and iPhones significantly enhances user experiences.

To know more, see [Setting up and using Oloid Connect for Passwordless](/passwordless/ios/connect/setting-up-and-using-oloid-connect-for-passwordless).
