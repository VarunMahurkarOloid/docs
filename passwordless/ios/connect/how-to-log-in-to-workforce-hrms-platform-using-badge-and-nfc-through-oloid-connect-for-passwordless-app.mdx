## Introduction

This document provides step-by-step instructions for logging in with badge and NFC to Workforce / HRMS platform.

## Pre-requisite

- User must have an Okta account.
- In the Oloid Portal, admin must do the following:Okta credential (user email ID) must be added to the user details page in Oloid Portal.Assign badge to the user. For more details, refer [How to assign Badge ID as credential to users](/administration/admin-portal/how-to-assign-badge-id-as-credential-to-users).Add NFC for the user. For more details, refer [How to assign NFC/ID as a credential to the user](/administration/admin-portal/how-to-assign-nfc-id-as-a-credential-to-the-user)Enable Badge option under the Oloid Verify application in Authentication tab. For more details, refer [How to create and configure Connect for Passwordless application.](/passwordless/ios/connect/how-to-create-and-configure-oloid-connect-passwordless-application-in-tenant-admin-portal)

## Using the Badge

1. Launch the Oloid Connect app on your iPad.
2. On the home page of the app, select **Badge Auth**. ![](/images/9789771/d481a4724662.png) The **Scan Badge at the Reader**screen is displayed. ![](/images/9789771/598837ade462.png)
3. Tap the **Badge** on the **Reader** to scan it.**Note:**The Card Reader should be connected to your iPad.
4. On Badge authentication, the **Tap NFC Tag at the Device**screen is displayed. ![](/images/9891142/37567bf50218.png)
5. Tap the **NFC tag** on the **Reader** to scan it.**Note:**The Reader should be connected to your iPad.On NFC authentication, you are signed in to the **Workforce /HRMS** platform. ![](/images/9891142/e1d30618319e.png)
