**Introduction:**  
To check hardware compatibility using the Oloid Connect app, you can perform a hardware check through Admin Mode to ensure the device meets the required specifications for proper functionality.

### Pre-requisite:

- The user must be a Tenant Admin or Supervisor.
- The endpoint must be paired.For more, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint)

### Steps to check hardware compatibility:

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect**![](/images/10288412/f7c4703dd471.png) **App**.**Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the initial screen of the App. ![](/images/10308108/bdb26aa81cf8.png)
2. Double tap with two fingers on the initial screen to launch the Admin Mode.
3. Tap **Yes- Continue.** ![](/images/10308108/a2078cd1bdd8.png) The **Settings**screen is displayed.
4. Tap **Start** in the **Hardware Check** section. ![](/images/10308108/2bf781fca551.png) The pop-up to **select Hardware Type** is displayed.
5. Select **Endpoint**. ![](/images/10308108/d8ba0ebc5e20.png) The **Hardware Check** screen is displayed to check the details. ![](/images/10308108/f6a7d85273cb.png)
