## Hardware requirement for Oloid Connect installation

## Hardware Requirement for iPad

| Model | iPad mini, iPad Pro (11- inch) and newer |
|---|---|
| Camera | True Depth Camera (Recommended) |
| Mount and Case for the iPad | Recommended for secure placement and durability |
| Offline Mode Support for FR | iPad mini, iPad Pro (11- inch) and newer |

## Hardware Requirement for iPhone

| Model | iPhone X and newer |
|---|---|
| Mount and Case for iPhone | Not applicable for iPhone. |
| Offline Mode Support for FR | iPhone X and newer |

## Software Requirement

The Oloid Connect application requires iOS 15 or later. To ensure compatibility, security, and performance, keep the iPad updated to the most recent supported iOS version.

## Electrical and Power Requirement

- 120V outlet access for charging device.
- PoE adapter, if the customer prefers a wired network connection.

## iPad Installation

For Installation of the iPad, the recommended height to mount the iPad is 5 feet. This is to cater to people with different heights. However, for employees for whom recommended height is too high, a separate iPad may be mounted at a lower height.

For more information, see [Oloid's iPAD Installation Guide](/passwordless/ios/connect/oloid-connect-verify-ipad-installation).

## Others

- Make sure there is enough light around the iPad so the user's face is clearly visible.
- iPad should be mounted in such a way that the camera of device is at 5 feet height from the floor.
