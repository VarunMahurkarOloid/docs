## Introduction

This document describes the ways to hardboot an iPAD PRO 11.

The document comprises the following:

- [How to restart an iPad without a Home button?](#h_a51e3bc211)
- [How to restart an iPad with a Home button?](#h_45f0ee3b50)

## How to restart an iPad without a Home button?

1.    Press and hold either volume button and the top button until the power off slider appears.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/6911789/57c5df2766b2.png" alt="Screenshot" />
</Frame>

2. Drag the slider, then wait 30 seconds for your device to turn off. If your device is frozen or unresponsive, [force restart your device](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/restart-ipad-ipad63d30b5a/14.0/ipados/14.0).

3. To turn your device back on, press and hold the top button until you see the Apple logo.

## How to restart an iPad with a Home button?

1. Press and hold the top button until the power off slider appears.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/6911789/c30e088577a2.png" alt="Screenshot" />
</Frame>

2. Drag the slider, then wait for 30 seconds for your device to turn off. If your device is frozen or unresponsive, [force restart your device](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/restart-ipad-ipad63d30b5a/14.0/ipados/14.0).

3. To turn your device back on, press and hold the top button until you see the Apple logo.
