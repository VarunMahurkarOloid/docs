**Oloid Vault (Chrome / Chromium)** is a chrome browser extension that helps you to create and securely stores new login credentials and simplifies auto filling your passwords. This extension provides a seamless experience for users who manage multiple credentials across various websites and services.

## Key Features

1. **Secure Storage**:Chrome Vault securely stores all your usernames and passwords, ensuring that your sensitive information is protected.
2. **User-Specific Data**:Each user's credentials are uniquely managed, providing personalized security for every account.
3. **Website-Specific Management**:The extension allows for the storage and organization of passwords specific to each website, making it easy to manage multiple accounts.
4. **Authentication Capabilities**:Chrome Vault includes advanced authentication features, adding an extra layer of security when accessing your stored credentials.
5. **Autofill Functionality**:With its autofill capabilities, Chrome Vault simplifies the process of accessing and filling in credentials, saving time and reducing the risk of errors.
6. **Enhanced Security**:By securely storing and managing your credentials, Chrome Vault enhances browsing security, protecting your information from unauthorized access.
7. **Convenience**:The extension streamlines the process of logging in to websites, providing a convenient and efficient browsing experience.
8. **Passkey:**Creates, stores, and manages FIDO2 passkeys and security keys, enabling seamless and secure passwordless authentication.

**Oloid's Chrome Vault** is an essential tool for anyone looking to improve their online security and streamline their login processes. With its combination of secure storage, authentication features, and autofill functionality, Chrome Vault offers a reliable solution for managing your credentials.
