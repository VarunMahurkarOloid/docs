## Introduction

Webkey allows users to enroll their face as a credential for face-based authentication. During enrollment, the user provides consent and captures an image that is associated with their profile for use during future authentication.

## Prerequisites

- User Image must be imported into HR Picture Verification. For more information, Refer: [How to Import Faces for HR Picture Verification](/administration/admin-portal/how-to-import-faces-for-hr-picture-verification)
- Manual face capture (as per the requirement) must be configured in Oloid Portal.
- Enable Enrollment field should be selected at the application level. Refer **Create and configure Webkey >**[Configure Customize Buttons tab](/administration/admin-portal/webkey-how-to-create-and-configure-a-webkey-application-in-tenant-admin-portal)

**To enroll for face login:**

1. Launch the **Webkey**Application URL in your browser.
2. Select **Enroll for Face Login**.**Note:**You can select preferred display language from the **Language Dropdown Selector**. For more information, refer [Additional information section](#h_82073d25e5). ![](/images/9263144/657429366c22.png) The **Enroll for Face Login**page is displayed.**Note:**In case, custom URL is added for your application then you will be redirected to the provided redirect URL. Follow the onscreen instructions to enroll your face.
3. Enter your **Employee ID** (primary ID).
4. Click **Continue**. ![](/images/9263144/c379d54c1b6a.png) TheConsentpage is displayed.
5. Click **Agree**. ![](/images/9263144/0eccc0fe4330.png) This opens the camera on your device.
6. Look at the camera to capture your face for enrollment and press **Enter or Spacebar.**Note: For manual capture to function it must be configured in the Tenant Admin Portal. ![](/images/9263144/f305ad525756.png)
7. Do one of the following:Tap **Yes- Submit** to submit the image.The face is submitted successfully and is displayed on the details screen.Tap **No- Retake** to take the photo again.The camera turns on to retake the photo.**Note**: You can add a maximum of five faces per user. The user's face is added to the Oloid portal. ![](/images/9263144/fe66a51e5872.png)

Face is enrolled successfully.

### Additional Information

**WebKey** now supports multiple language versions, allowing users to select their preferred display language from the **Language Dropdown Selector** on the Login screen.

**Note:** Languages supported: English, Spanish, French, German, and Portuguese language.
