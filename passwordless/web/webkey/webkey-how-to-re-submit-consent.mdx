## Introduction

Re-submit Consent ensures that users explicitly acknowledge and accept updated consent documents before continuing with face-based authentication or enrollment. When the consent content is modified, users are prompted to re-consent.

## Prerequisite

Resubmit-consent pop-up will only appear if the consent document is updated.

## Steps to re-submit consent

Reconsent Document must be submitted for the following cases:

1. [For login using face authentication](#h_06c1347f87)(if the consent document has been updated)
2. [For Enrolling Face](#h_094b76baac) (if the consent document has been updated)

## Re-submit consent for login using face authentication

1. Launch the **Webkey**Application URL in your browser.
2. Select **Login with Face**.**Note:**You can select preferred display language from the **Language Dropdown Selector**. For more information, refer [Additional information section](#h_35f2e41fcc). ![](/images/13165653/c0c9d60a8497.png) The camera opens.
3. Position your face in front of the camera and tap the Capture button to authenticate face.**Note**: If you do not tap the Capture button, the app automatically captures your face after a few seconds. ![](/images/13165653/f305ad525756.png)
4. The re-submit consent screen appears.
5. Click **Agree.** ![](/images/13165653/0eccc0fe4330.png) **Note:** You need to provide your consent to proceed to next steps if consent is enabled for you. Face is successfully authenticated and user is taken to their organization account.

---

## Re-submit consent  to Enroll for Face

1. Launch the WebKey Application URL in your browser.
2. Select **Enroll for Face Login**. ![](/images/13165653/1cd381da1fbe.png) The **Enroll For Face Login**pageisdisplayed.
3. Enter the Employee ID (primary ID) > click **Continue**. ![](/images/13165653/c379d54c1b6a.png) The camera opens.
4. Position your face in front of the camera to capture your face for enrollment. If auto capture is enabled the system captures user image directly. **Note:** If auto capture is not configured press **Enter or Spacebar** to capture face. ![](/images/13165653/f305ad525756.png) The Picture confirmation screen is displayed.
5. Click **Yes** to Submit. ![](/images/13165653/fe66a51e5872.png) The Re-submit consent page is displayed.
6. Click **Agree**.

**Note:** You need to provide your consent to proceed to next steps if consent is enabled for you.

**WebKey** now supports multiple language versions, allowing users to select their preferred display language from the **Language Dropdown Selector** on the Login screen.

**Note:** Languages supported: English, Spanish, French, German, and Portuguese language.
