OloID CloudKey app provides an easy contactless way to authenticate and provide secure access over your mobile. With the app, you can easily unlock the door with a tap.

OloID CloudKey app is compatible with all mobile devices. Below is a condensed list of compatible devices.

- Android phones with OS 6 and above

- iPhone with OS 12 and above

**Pre-requisite**: You should have a valid OloID account.

## Installation Procedure

1. Go to **App Store** or **Play Store** and download **OloID CloudKey App.**
2. Install the application and upon successful installation, open the app.
3. Click **Get Started**. ![](/images/9310637/0225fff747cd.png)
4. Enter **Email ID**and Click **Next**on the **Sign In** screen. ![](/images/9310637/ec6af9c4d5a3.png)
5. Enter **Company Code** and click **Apply**. ![](/images/9310637/57498fd6360d.png) The Tenant is listed on the screen. ![](/images/9310637/a538f2b4f85d.png)
6. Tap on the **Tenant** and click **Apply**. ![](/images/9310637/0911b2ce5517.png)
7. Enter your registered email id and click **Next**.**Note**: You can also use SSO login. ![](/images/9310637/a151554b74a0.png) A 6-digit code is sent to the registered email.
8. Enter the **6-digit code**and tap **Next** to successfully log in. ![](/images/9310637/775f569d7bd3.png)
9. Click **Continue** when OloID CloudKey App prompts for Bluetooth, location, and push notification permissions.![](/images/9310637/c0e2c3f82bb0.png) ![](/images/9310637/20a7610f8603.png) ![](/images/9310637/ca93016ac219.png) The Welcome screen is displayed. ![](/images/9310637/82e1911e061d.png) The **Wallet** screen is displayed.
