To share logs with developer:

1. Launch the **OLOID CloudKey app.**
2. Log in using your registered email ID. ![](/images/9316986/2a982ceaa866.png) The **Wallet** screen with a list of doors is displayed. ![](/images/9310874/3dbf92bd9b2e.png)
3. Tap on the **Profile tab** at the bottom right of the screen. ![](/images/9316986/98bc777df791.png)
4. Scroll down to the bottom of the screen and tap **Settings**. ![](/images/9316986/5a6a62f9efce.png)The **Settings**screen is displayed.
5. On the Settings screen, tap **Share logs with developer**. ![](/images/9316986/1388ef7d07a2.png) The logs are uploaded and shared with the developer.
