Getting Started with OLOID CloudKey App

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/9310846/d91ef72b064f.png" alt="Screenshot" />
</Frame>

Download OloID CloudKey App : [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.oloID.smartkeys&hl=en_IN&gl=US) or [Apple iOS](https://apps.apple.com/ro/app/oloid-cloudkey/id6479234080)

OloID CloudKey app provides an easy contactless way to authenticate and provide secure access over your mobile. The OloID CloudKey app allows you to unlock a door.

The OloID CloudKey app works only with _CloudKey-enabled doors._

OloID CloudKey app is compatible with all mobile devices. Below is a condensed list of compatible devices.

- Android phones with OS 6 and above
- iPhone with OS 12 and above
