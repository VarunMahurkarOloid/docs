## Introduction

This document provides instructions to log in to the **OloID CloudKey App** using **Azure**.

## Pre-requisite

- The App user should have a verified **Microsoft (Azure)** email account.

- The App user should be an Oloid user. For more information on this, please **contact your admin.**

## OloID CloudKey App Login using Azure

To log in to the **OloID CloudKey App** using **Azure**, follow the below steps:

1. Launch the **OloID CloudKey App**. (If App is not present, download the app from **App Store** / **Play Store**).
2. Enter the **Microsoft email ID** in the **Sign in** screen and click **Next**. ![](/images/9310861/c0819f3166b2.png)
3. Select a specific **Account**from the tenant list**.** ![](/images/9310861/c1f51a49a1cc.png) Tap the desired account to proceed further.
4. Click **Continue with Microsoft**. ![](/images/9310861/9968cb19280a.png)
5. Click **Continue** when **OloID CloudKey** App prompts for permission to use the Microsoft account for logging in to the app. ![](/images/9310861/b0ce542f078a.png) The account is listed on the screen.
6. Click on the account.**Note**: If you have multiple accounts listed, select the same email ID entered in step 2 above. ![](/images/9310861/1440bcddb1f2.png)
7. Enter your credentials to successfully log in.
