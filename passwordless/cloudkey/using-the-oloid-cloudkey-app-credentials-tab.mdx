To view Credentials tab:

1. Launch the **OLOID CloudKey** app.
2. Tap **Credentials** on the menu at the bottom of the screen to see your credentials. ![](/images/9316981/e648ea859a46.png) The **Credentials** screen is displayed, showing the status of the CloudKey credential card. ![](/images/9316981/e1ab93375a50.png)

<Note>
  The CloudKey credential card is not displayed if it is not generated. The card status shows up as inactive if the credential has expired.
</Note>

Contact **Tenant Admin** to generate CloudKey credential card or extend the expiry date.
