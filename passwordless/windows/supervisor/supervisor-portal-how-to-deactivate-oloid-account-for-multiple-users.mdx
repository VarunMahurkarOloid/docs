Steps to de-activate Oloid Account:

1. Log in to the **Supervisor Portal**.The **Users** page is displayed.
2. Select user(s) to de-activate Oloid Account. ![](/images/9711899/13c70186e36f.png)
3. Go to **Action > De-Activate Oloid Account**. ![](/images/9711899/925b400a9a2e.png) The Oloid account is deactivated for the selected users. ![](/images/9711899/0ce8131ec678.png)
