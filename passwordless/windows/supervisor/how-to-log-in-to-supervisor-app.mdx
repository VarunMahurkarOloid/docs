### Steps to log in to Supervisor App:

1. Double-click on the **Supervisor App** to start. ![](/images/9036798/7be9650e5076.png)
2. Enter your login credentials in the appropriate fields. ![](/images/9037188/ebe11383961b.png) **Note**: If this is your first login after installing the application, please enter your Tenant Name and pair the endpoint.Refer [How to pair endpoint using Pair as Supervisor in Supervisor App](/passwordless/windows/supervisor/windows-login-how-to-manually-pair-the-supervisor-app)
3. Click **Login**.The Admin Home screen is displayed. ![](/images/9037188/f47cd43a998c.png)
