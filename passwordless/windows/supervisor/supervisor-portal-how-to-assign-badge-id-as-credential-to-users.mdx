Steps to Assign Badge:

1. Log in to the **Supervisor Portal.**The Users page is displayed.
2. Click **username**to edit. ![Image](/images/9679401/f4e342d85259.png) The User details page is displayed. ![](/images/9679401/98ba8477b3d0.png)
3. Do one of the following:Go to**Credentials**> **Badge**> **+** **Assign Badge**. ![Image](/images/9679401/b457db536531.png) Go to **Assign Credential**> **Assign Badge**. ![Image](/images/9679401/d29af910e947.png) The **Assign Badge** pop-up box is displayed.
4. On the pop up box, enter **Badge ID** or **Secondary ID**, then select **Wiegand Format**. ![](/images/9679401/d8256914c4a2.png)
5. Click **Assign**.The badge ID is assigned and listed on the **Badge** tab. ![Image](/images/9679401/fa0d27a762d7.png) Video TutorialWatch the video [How to assign Badge ID as credential to Users in the Supervisor Portal - Video Tutorial](/passwordless/windows/supervisor/how-to-assign-badge-id-as-credential-to-users-in-the-supervisor-portal-video-tutorial)
