Steps to create PIN:

1. Log in to the **Supervisor Portal.**The **Users**page is displayed.
2. Click **username** to edit. ![](/images/9674152/d3cb6459c8bb.png) The user details page is displayed. ![](/images/9674277/fba518291a49.png)
3. Do one of the following:Go to**Credentials**> **PIN**> **Add PIN**. ![Image](/images/9674277/bba7253f7573.png) Go to **Assign Credential**> **Add PIN**. ![Image](/images/9674277/5d87850cf075.png) The **Add PIN**pop-up box is displayed. ![Image](/images/9674277/8db1ec8a707d.png)
4. On the pop-up box, do the following:Enter **new PIN**.**Note**: You must enter numbers.Re-enter **new PIN**.Under **Share PIN With**enter email address you want to share the **pin** with.Click **Save**. ![Image](/images/9674277/f263f063643a.png) The PIN is created and listed on the **PIN**tab.
