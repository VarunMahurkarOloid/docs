Steps to Invite to Add Face:

1. Log in to the **Supervisor Portal**.The **Users**page is displayed.
2. Click **username** to edit. ![Image](/images/10304522/d07eb41c2813.png) The user details page is displayed.
3. Go to **Credentials** > **Face**> **Invite to Add Face**. ![](/images/9673759/063782c95789.png) An email invite link is sent to user to upload their face. To upload face using the invite link, see **[How to add face using the invite link](/administration/user-portal/how-to-add-a-face-using-invite-link-in-oloid-user-portal)**[.](/administration/user-portal/how-to-add-a-face-using-invite-link-in-oloid-user-portal) Video TutorialWatch the video [How to invite to add Face in the Supervisor Portal - Video Tutorial](https://oloid.help/en/articles/9902212-how-to-invite-to-add-face-in-the-supervisor-portal-video-tutorial) ​
