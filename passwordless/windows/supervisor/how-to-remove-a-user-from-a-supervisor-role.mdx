Steps to remove someone as a supervisor:

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the homepage of the portal, click **user profile icon**on the bottom left corner of the menu panel. ![](/images/10299914/c8560f399688.png) The user profile menu is displayed.
3. Click **Tenant** **Settings**. ![](/images/8231496/b5a63d6e40d4.png) The **Tenant Settings** page is displayed.
4. Go to **Admin Controls** > **Supervisors**. ![Image](/images/8231496/5c1f1f5afb4c.png)
5. Click **kebab menu** (three vertical dots) of the supervisor to be removed and select **Delete Supervisor**. ![Image](/images/8231496/4f27e2e1badf.png) The **Remove Supervisor** pop-up box is displayed. ![](/images/8231496/95d9c3aabefa.png)
6. Click **Delete**.The supervisor's rights are removed for the user. The user will no longer have access to the Oloid Verify/Connect app in admin mode. ![](/images/8231496/0c60477aa94b.png)
