To log out:

1. Log in to **Supervisor**App.The **Admin Home** screen is displayed.**Note**: The app is already paired with the endpoint.
2. On the bottom right, select **More Options > Logout**.![](/images/9063736/3cec524be287.png) You are successfully logged out of the Supervisor App.
