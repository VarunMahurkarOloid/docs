Steps to add user:

1. Log in to the **Supervisor Portal.**The Users page is displayed.
2. Click **Create User**from the top right corner. ![](/images/9669198/4bd0964fa2ad.png) The **Create User** pop-up box is displayed. ![](/images/9669198/052af1a98c80.png)
3. On the pop-up box, do the following:Enter **Full Name** of the user.Enter **Primary ID**.Select **Use OloID as Primary ID** to use OloID as your primary ID.Select**Connection** from the**Connection** list.Click **Create**.The new user is added and listed on the **Users** page. ![](/images/9669198/df4f75dd9f78.png)
