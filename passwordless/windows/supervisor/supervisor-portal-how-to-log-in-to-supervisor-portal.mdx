The Supervisor Portal is a restricted area within Oloid's Tenant Admin Portal, offering supervisors limited access as defined by the admin.

**Pre-requisite**: You should be a user with Supervisor role.

1. Launch your browser.
2. Enter the URL provided for you in the browser’s address bar. Then, press **Enter** on your keyboard.The Supervisor Portal login page is displayed. ![](/images/9690901/5a38e52d9d7d.png)
3. Do the following:Enter your email address in the **Email** field.Enter **Password**.Click **Login**.The Oloid Supervisor Portal is displayed.**Note**: Oloid uses **SSO login** and also allows you to log in to the portal with your Google, Microsoft, Okta, or with your Email account.
