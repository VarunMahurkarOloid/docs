## Introduction

NFC/ID is assigned for the DeviceLock app to function.

Steps to assign NFC/ID:

1. Log in to the **Supervisor Portal.** The Users page is displayed.
2. Click username to edit. ![](/images/9673945/d3cb6459c8bb.png) The user details page is displayed. ![](/images/9673945/fba518291a49.png)
3. Do one of the following:Go to **Credentials** > **NFC/ID**tab> **+** **Assign NFC/ID**. ![Image](/images/9673945/9e1a69cf3cdf.png) Go to **Assign Credential** > **Assign NFC/ID**. ![Image](/images/9673945/d6153703a148.png) The **Assign NFC/ID**pop-up box is displayed. ![](/images/9673945/8a401364577d.png)
4. On the pop-up box, do the following:Enter your unique **NFC/ID**security number.**Note**: You can tap the **NFC/ID**tag on the **Reader** to scan it.Click **Assign**.The NFC/ID number is assigned and listed on the **NFC/ID** tab. ![Image](/images/9673945/70dbce73222d.png) Video TutorialWatch the video[How to assign NFC/ID as a credential to the user in the Supervisor Portal- Video Tutorial](/passwordless/windows/supervisor/how-to-assign-nfc-id-as-a-credential-to-the-user-in-the-supervisor-portal-video-tutorial) ​
