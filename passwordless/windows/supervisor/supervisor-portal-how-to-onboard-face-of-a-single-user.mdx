Steps to add face of a single user:

1. Log in to the **Supervisor Portal.**The **Users** page is displayed.
2. Click **username** to edit. ![](/images/9669583/d3cb6459c8bb.png) Theuser details page is displayed. ![](/images/9669583/cc59d6c44d9b.png)
3. Do one of the following:Go to**Credentials**> **Face**> + **Add Face**. ![Image](/images/9669583/412e29cf930d.png) Go to **Assign Credential**> **Add Face**. ![Image](/images/9669583/8fe0c085ba6e.png) The pop-up box to upload image is displayed. ![](/images/9669583/9b16b233a1d1.png)
4. Click or Drag and Drop the image to be uploaded.
5. Click **Upload**.The image is uploaded and listed on the **Face** tab. ![](/images/9669583/0b0f1c0120f0.png) **Note**: You can add a maximum of five faces per user.
