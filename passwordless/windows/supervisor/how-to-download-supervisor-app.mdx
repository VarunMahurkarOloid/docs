The Supervisor App can be downloaded from Tenant Settings section in the Tenant Admin Portal.

## Steps to download from Tenant Settings

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the homepage of the portal, click **User profile icon**on the bottom left corner of the menu panel. ![](/images/15457971/24b74e59a157.png) The user profile menu is displayed.
3. Select **Tenant Settings**. ![](/images/13713819/c88e8e3b0807.png) The **Tenant Settings**screen is displayed. ![](/images/15457971/eeb6270f08ee.png)
4. Go to **Oloid Apps**tab > **Windows Authenticator App**. ![](/images/9036607/4d5a00cb16d5.png)
5. Select the preferred version from the **Win-Login-Installer** dropdown list, then click the **Download** button![](/images/9036607/ad510ed335f9.png)icon. ![](/images/9036607/2977a07903f3.png) The installer file is downloaded to your local device.
