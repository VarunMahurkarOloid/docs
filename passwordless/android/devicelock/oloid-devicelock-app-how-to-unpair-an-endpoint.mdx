## Introduction

The Unpair Endpoint feature in the Oloid DeviceLock App allows admins to disconnect an endpoint that is no longer in use or requires reconfiguration.

## Prerequisite

The Endpoint must be paired. To pair refer [How to pair a DeviceLock endpoint](/passwordless/android/devicelock/oloid-devicelock-app-ways-to-pair-an-endpoint).

## Steps to Unpair Endpoint

1. Launch **Oloid DeviceLock App** on your device. ![](/images/10329310/d3bb63126deb.png) The welcome screen is displayed.
2. Login to the Admin mode in **DeviceLock App.**To login refer[How to log in to admin mode in DeviceLock App](/passwordless/android/devicelock/oloid-devicelock-app-how-to-log-in-to-admin-mode).The **Settings** screen is displayed.
3. Tap **Unpair**. ![](/images/10329310/ccf55ae97f48.png) The **Confirm Unpair** pop-up box is displayed.
4. Tap **Unpair**. ![](/images/10329310/ffd8e954d2b3.png) The device is unpaired successfully. ![](/images/10329310/7406820ff7fb.png)
