## Introduction

The **Oloid DeviceLock app** enables users to securely unlock their Android devices using **NFC Tag credential**. After successful authentication, the device unlocks and redirects to the device **home screen**.

## Prerequisites

- Android device must be connected to an external NFC reader/card reader.**Note:** If the Android device has a **built-in NFC reader**, you can scan the tag directly on the device.
- You must have your assigned NFC ID.

## Steps to Unlock the Device

1. Press the **Power** button or tap the screen on your device.**Note:** The Device Lock app is displayed automatically, as it always runs in Kiosk Mode.The Welcome screen is displayed.
2. Tap **Login with NFC Tag**.**Note**: You will only see the authentication methods (Face, PIN, QR Code, Password and NFC) enabled for you. Button names may vary based on configuration. Contact [Support](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) if no authentication method appears. ![](/images/12779720/c1363931d6f8.png) **Tap NFC Tag at the device**pageis displayed.
3.
