## Introduction

The **Oloid DeviceLock app** enables users to securely unlock their Android devices using **Face credential**. After successful authentication, the device unlocks and redirects to the device **home screen**.

## Prerequisite

- The device must have a functional front-facing camera.

## Steps to Unlock the Device

1. Press the **Power** button or tap the screen on your device.**Note:** The Device Lock app is displayed automatically, as it always runs in Kiosk Mode.The Welcome screen is displayed.
2. Tap **Login with Face**.**Note**: You will only see the authentication methods (Face, Badge, PIN, QR Code, Password and NFC/ID) enabled for you. Button names may vary based on configuration. Contact [Support](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) if no authentication method appears. ![](/images/12779596/0ee1372075d5.png) The camera turns on.
3. Position your face in front of the camera and tap the **Capture** button to complete the authentication process.**Note:** If you do not tap the **Capture** button, the app automatically captures your face after a few seconds. ![](/images/12779596/31a0f485525c.png) After successful authentication, the device unlocks and displays the **home screen**. ![](/images/12779596/c6e75c8d26fe.png) Keywords| DeviceLock Face Authentication | Unlock Android device using Face | How to unlock device with Face in DeviceLock |
