## Introduction

The exitfeature in the **Oloid DeviceLock App** allows admin to safely close the application while in **Admin Mode**. When the app is exited, the admin is automatically logged out and returned to the device home screen.

## Prerequisite

You must have **Tenant Admin access** to the **Oloid Tenant Admin Portal**.

## Steps to Exit the DeviceLock Application

1. Launch **Oloid DeviceLock App** on your device. ![](/images/10329310/d3bb63126deb.png) The welcome screen is displayed.
2. Log in to Admin mode in **DeviceLock**App. For more details refer [How to log in to admin mode in DeviceLock App](/passwordless/android/devicelock/oloid-devicelock-app-how-to-log-in-to-admin-mode).
3. Tap **More option**icon (![](/images/11484484/d1ee5184bf7e.png)) on bottom-right side of the screen> Tap **Exit App**.**Note**: By Tapping **Exit App**, you will be logged out and redirected to the **home**screen of the device. ![](/images/11484484/3baa07379e2a.png) The Oloid DeviceLock App exited successfully. ![](/images/11484484/ea8f944c8aa4.png) Keywords| Exit Oloid DeviceLock App | Oloid DeviceLock App logout | Close DeviceLock App securely |
