## Introduction

This document provides step-by-step instructions to download and install the **DeviceLock** application from the Google Play Store on an Android device.

## Prerequisite

- Make sure you have a stable internet connection.
- You have access to the **Google Play Store** on the Android device.

## Steps to Download DeviceLock App

1. Locate the **Play Store** on your home screen and tap on it to launch the application. ![](/images/10062644/f24e93df1ad0.png)
2. Tap on the **Search** icon located at the bottom right corner of the screen.The Search bar is displayed.
3. In the search bar at the top of the screen, type **Oloid Device Lock** and tap on the search result. ![](/images/10062644/faf2c099d762.png)
4. Select **Oloid Device Lock** app. ![](/images/10062644/b92d7f420261.png)
5. Review the app's details, ratings, and user reviews to make sure it's the correct app you want to download. ![](/images/10062644/addb90654b90.png)
6. Tap the **Install**button located next to the **Oloid Device Lock** app icon. ![](/images/10062644/d5340a502a92.png) The **DeviceLock** app is installed successfully on the Android device.Once the installation is complete, launch the **Oloid Device Lock** app to open.
