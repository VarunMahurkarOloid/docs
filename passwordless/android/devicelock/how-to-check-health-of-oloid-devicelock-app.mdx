**Steps to check health:**

1. Launch **Oloid DeviceLock App** on your device. ![](/images/11507892/d3bb63126deb.png)
2. Do any one of the following:Select **More Option**icon (![](/images/11484484/d1ee5184bf7e.png)) on bottom-right side of the screen.ORDouble tap on the screen. ![](/images/11508048/95022e25471c.png) The Enter Admin Mode pop-up box is displayed.
3. Select **Continue**. ![](/images/11508048/c5bf6df85d71.png)
4. Select **Start**to initiate the **health check**. ![](/images/11508048/d8e455a861c6.png) Logs are successfully shared with Firebase. ![](/images/11508048/f9fd56a2d3b8.png)
