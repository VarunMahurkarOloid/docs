## Introduction

The **Logout** feature in the **Oloid DeviceLock app** allows administrators and supervisors to securely exit Admin Mode after completing configurations or management tasks. Logging out ensures that unauthorized users cannot access administrative settings on the device.

## Prerequisite

You must have **Admin access** to the **DeviceLock app**.

## Steps to log out of Oloid Devicelock App

1. Launch **Oloid DeviceLock App** on your device. ![](/images/11501456/d3bb63126deb.png) The welcome screen is displayed.
2. Log in to the **admin mode** in **DeviceLock**App. For more details, refer [How to log in to admin mode in DeviceLock App](/passwordless/android/devicelock/oloid-devicelock-app-how-to-log-in-to-admin-mode).
3. Tap **Logout icon** (![](/images/11501456/9f342723232b.png)) on the top-right side of the screen.**Note:** You can exit **Admin Mode** from any screen, as the top header with the Logout icon remains fixed and always accessible. ![](/images/11501456/1a89a8937592.png) The **Confirm Logout**pop-up box is displayed.
4. Tap **Logout**. ![](/images/11501456/d9b56b63e457.png) You will be redirected to the **Welcome screen**. ![](/images/11501456/5177b5adbe72.png) You have successfully logged out from the DeviceLock application. Keywords
