**Steps to share logs:**

1. Launch **Oloid DeviceLock App** on your device. ![](/images/11507892/d3bb63126deb.png)
2. Do any one of the following:Select **More Option**icon (![](/images/11484484/d1ee5184bf7e.png)) on bottom-right side of the screen.ORDouble tap on the screen. ![](/images/11507892/95022e25471c.png) The **Enter Admin Mode** pop-up box is displayed.
3. Select **Continue**. ![](/images/11507892/c5bf6df85d71.png)
4. Select **Send**.![](/images/11507892/77b2fca87acf.png)
5. Enter the recipient email address > click **Send**icon (![](/images/11507892/91892168aab0.png)) on the top-right side of the screen. ![](/images/11507892/305be60de924.png) The DeviceLock logs file is successfully sent to the recipient's email address.
