## Introduction

The **Login with Badge and PIN** feature in the **Oloid DeviceLock App** allows users to securely access their **Workforce/HRMS platform** through a two-step authentication process. Users first tap their **Badge**on the device to verify their identity, followed by entering their **PIN** to complete the login.

## Prerequisites

- Ensure that the Android device is connected to an external card reader.
- You must have a valid Okta account.
- You must have your assigned Badge ID.
- You must have your assigned PIN.

## Steps to Log in with Badge and Pin

1. Press the **Power** button or tap the screen on your device.**Note:** The Device Lock app is displayed automatically, as it always runs in Kiosk Mode.The Welcome screen is displayed.
2. Tap **Login with Badge**. ![](/images/12779676/6e54eab4e173.png) The **Scan Badge at the Reader**pop-up box is displayed.
3. Tap the Badge on the Badge Reader to scan it. ![](/images/12779676/53c31be2904f.png) After successful Badge authentication, the **Enter PIN**pop-up box is displayed.
4.
