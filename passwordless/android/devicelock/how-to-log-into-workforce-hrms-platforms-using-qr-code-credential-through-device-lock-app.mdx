## Introduction

The **Login with QR Code** feature in the **Oloid DeviceLock App** allows users to securely access their **Workforce/HRMS platform** by scanning a QR code linked to their **credentials**. This method provides a quick and secure way to authenticate without entering a password.

## Prerequisites

- You must have a valid Okta account.
- A valid QR code must be assigned to you.

## Steps to Log in with QR Code

1. Press the **Power** button or tap the screen on your device.**Note:** The Device Lock app is displayed automatically, as it always runs in Kiosk Mode.The Welcome screen is displayed.
2. Tap **Login with QR**. **Note**: You will only see the authentication methods (Face, Badge, PIN, QR Code and NFC/ID) enabled for you. Button names may vary based on configuration. Contact [Support](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) if no authentication method appears. ![](/images/10026016/2266675129b1.png) The camera turns on.
3. Show your QR Code in front of the camera to capture the QR code. ![](/images/10026016/6e1eec47b5ca.png) After successful authentication, you are signed in to the Okta system. ![](/images/10026016/180530975f34.png)
