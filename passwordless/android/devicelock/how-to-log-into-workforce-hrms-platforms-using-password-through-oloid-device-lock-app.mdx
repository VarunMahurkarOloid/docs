## Introduction

The **Login with Password** feature in the Oloid DeviceLock App allows users to securely access their **Workforce/HRMS platform** using their credentials.

## Prerequisite

- You must have a valid Okta account.
- You must have your DeviceLock credentials.

## Steps to Log in with Password

1. Press the **Power** button or tap the screen on your device.**Note:** The Device Lock app is displayed automatically, as it always runs in Kiosk Mode.The Welcome screen is displayed.
2. Tap **Login with Password**.**Note**: You will only see the authentication methods (Face, Badge, PIN, QR Code and NFC/ID) enabled for you. Button names may vary based on configuration. Contact [Support](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) if no authentication method appears. ![](/images/8707338/7f3eba78bfcf.png) The Login with Password screen is displayed.
3. Enter your login credentials in the respective fields and tap on **Login**. ![](/images/8707338/3d3c403cfde6.png) You are successfully signed in to the Okta system. ![](/images/8403260/db3e6ae9fb7a.png)
