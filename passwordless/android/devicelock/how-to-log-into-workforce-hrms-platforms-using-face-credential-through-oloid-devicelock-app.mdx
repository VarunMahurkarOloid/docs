## Introduction

The Oloid DeviceLock App allows users to log in securely using facial recognition. With Face credentials, users can access their Workforce/HRMS account quickly, ensuring a secure and contactless login experience.

## Prerequisite

You must have a valid Okta account.

## Steps to Log in with Face

1. Press the **Power** button or tap the screen on your device.**Note:** The Device Lock app is displayed automatically, as it always runs in Kiosk Mode.The Welcome screen is displayed.
2. Tap **Login with Face**.**Note**: You will only see the authentication methods (Face, Badge, PIN, QR Code, Password and NFC/ID) enabled for you. Button names may vary based on configuration. Contact [Support](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) if no authentication method appears. ![](/images/12779596/0ee1372075d5.png) The camera turns on.
3. Position your face in front of the camera and tap the **Capture** button to complete the authentication process.**Note:** If you do not tap the **Capture** button, the app automatically captures your face after a few seconds. ![](/images/12779596/31a0f485525c.png) After successful authentication, you are signed in to the Okta system. ![](/images/8702335/8622540e5370.png)
