## Introduction

The **Oloid DeviceLock app** enables users to securely unlock their Android devices using **Badge credential**. After successful authentication, the device unlocks and redirects to the device **home screen**.

## Prerequisites

- Ensure that the Android device is connected to an external card reader.
- You must have your assigned Badge ID.

## Steps to Unlock the Device

1. Press the **Power** button or tap the screen on your device.**Note:** The Device Lock app is displayed automatically, as it always runs in Kiosk Mode.The Welcome screen is displayed.
2. Tap **Login with Badge**.**Note**: You will only see the authentication methods (Face, Badge, PIN, QR, Password and NFC/ID) enabled for you. Button names may vary based on configuration. Contact [Support](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) if no authentication method appears. ![](/images/12779676/6e54eab4e173.png) The **Scan Badge at the Reader**pop-up box is displayed.
3.
