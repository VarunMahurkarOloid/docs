## Introduction

The **Login with NFC and PIN** feature in the **Oloid DeviceLock App** allows users to securely access their **Workforce/HRMS platform** through a two-step authentication process. Users first tap their **NFC tag** on the device to verify their identity, followed by entering their **PIN** to complete the login.

## Prerequisites

- Android device must be connected to an external NFC reader/card reader.**Note:** If the Android device has a **built-in NFC reader**, you can scan the tag directly on the device.
- You must have a valid Okta account.
- You must have your assigned NFC ID.
- You must have your assigned PIN.

## Steps to Log in with NFC and PIN

1. Press the **Power** button or tap the screen on your device.**Note:** The Device Lock app is displayed automatically, as it always runs in Kiosk Mode.The Welcome screen is displayed.
2. Tap **Login with NFC Tag**.**Note**: You will only see the authentication methods (Face, Badge, PIN, QR Code, Password and NFC/ID) enabled for you. Button names may vary based on configuration. Contact [Support](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) if no authentication method appears. ![](/images/12779720/c1363931d6f8.png) **Tap NFC Tag at the device**pageis displayed.
3. Scan the NFC Tag. ![](/images/12779720/b1d944d330c2.png) After successful NFC authentication, the **Enter PIN**page is displayed.
4. Enter the **PIN**.
