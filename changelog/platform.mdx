**Tenant Admin Portal** serves as the management portal for creating and managing users, applications, connections, groups, and credentials within the Oloid system. It enables administrators to configure authentication methods, integrate with identity providers, monitor system activity through reports, and perform other critical administrative tasks.

# RELEASE UPDATE - MID-DECEMBER 2025

## What's New?

### Updated Email Templates for Invites and Password Recovery

The Invite and Forgot Password email templates have been updated across all tenants to improve clarity and consistency.

#### ​Key updates include:

- The From email address has been updated from admin@oloid.ai to no_reply@oloid.ai for Admin Invite and Forgot Password emails.

- Email templates now include the Tenant name, allowing users to easily identify the source of the email.

- These updates apply to emails sent from both the Admin Portal and User Portal. Note: No action is required from admin. The updated templates are applied automatically to all tenants.

## Endpoints

### "Back Online" Email Notification for Endpoints

A "Back Online” email notification is sent when an endpoint returns to an online state after being offline.

​This improvement helps admin to:

- Quickly identify when endpoints are operational again.
- Monitor the endpoint availability without performing manual checks.
