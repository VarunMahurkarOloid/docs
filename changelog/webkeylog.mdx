**Oloid's WebKey** is a browser-based application that enables Multi-factor authentication (MFA) of users using physical identity factors (like Face, Badge, NFC tags, QR Code, and Pin). This application allows employees to seamlessly log into Workforce/HRMS systems without entering a Username and Password.

  Tenant Admins can now enable manual capture for facial recognition in the WebKey application.  
  Instead of the app automatically capturing the face, users can click the camera icon, or press **Enter** or **Spacebar**, to take a photo.
