![Alt text](/images/oloid-android.jpg)

**Oloid's Android App** provides employees with a secure and seamless way to log into Workforce/HRMS Platforms and Windows endpoints using physical identity factors (Face, Badge, NFC tags, QR Code, and PIN). The app also includes powerful admin capabilities for managing users, credentials, and endpoint configurations.
